What we keep

  • Your account: the email and name you sign in with. Sign-in is handled by Auth0, which keeps your password; we never see it.
  • What you make: your quilts, fabric photos, photos of your real quilts, folders, and notes on your fabrics (how much you have, where it's kept).
  • What you share: a quilt you share with the Community, with its fabric photos, its realistic picture and the name you share under. Anyone can see these, signed in or not.
  • Reports and blocks: quilts you report, and people whose quilts you've chosen not to see.
  • The web page's error reports: if the web page fails, a short technical report goes to Sentry, without your quilts, names, email or photo links. The phone app doesn't send these.

Photos and the camera

The app uses the camera and your photos only when you ask it to, to add a picture of a quilt or fabric. Before keeping a photo, the app makes a new copy of it, which leaves behind what the camera recorded, such as where it was taken. Photos of your real quilts are private: only you can see them.

Why we keep it

To keep your work safe, bring it to your other devices, and show what you choose to share. We don't sell your data, we don't show advertising, and we don't use your quilts or photos for anything else. [Owner to confirm the legal basis under Brazil's LGPD.]
