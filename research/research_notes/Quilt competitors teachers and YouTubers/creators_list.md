## Who are the main Brazilian patchwork/quilting creators on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok?

### Takeaway
Brazil's biggest patchwork reach sits with a handful of beginner-oriented YouTube teachers: Arte de Paninho (~845K YouTube / ~570K Instagram, broader "costura criativa"), Ana Cosentino (320K YouTube, ~145K IG) and Natália Engel Studio (~229K YouTube), followed by long-time teachers Tia Lili (~65K IG) and Márcia Satiko; almost all of them already sell their own courses (Hotmart or own site) and many run a shop, so they are used to earning from their audience, which fits a revenue-share model but also means the app must complement, not compete with, their courses. Machine-brand ties exist only at the art-quilt end (Estela Mota with Bernina, Carolina Oneto with Janome), and no Brazilian patchwork presence on TikTok showed up at all.

### Inferences
- Tier 1 Brazilian targets by reach: Arte de Paninho, Ana Cosentino, Natália Engel. Ana Cosentino and Tia Lili are the closest fit for a design app (traditional blocks, samplers, rulers, fabric lines), Natália Engel for "beginner block" audiences, Arte de Paninho is broader (bags, dolls, gifts), so the app's bag/accessory value would need to be shown to her audience.
- Because most of these creators already sell courses and fabric, a pitch that bundles the app into their course (students get the blocks of the course pre-drawn with cutting lists) and fills carts in *their* shop is likely more attractive than a plain affiliate link. Ana Cosentino (Alecrim Tecidos) and Tia Lili (Pano Xadrez) both own a fabric/ruler shop that the cart-filling feature could point to.
- Ana Cosentino's flat subscriber count since 2023 suggests a mature, older audience; recurring revenue share may appeal as a new income line.
- Machine brands (Bernina, Janome) work with art quilters (Estela Mota, Carolina Oneto) rather than the big beginner channels in what was found; these two have prestige among teachers more than mass reach.
- Brazilian patchwork on TikTok appears negligible; YouTube + Instagram + Hotmart is the ecosystem.

### Gaps
- No subscriber counts found for Tia Lili's YouTube, Márcia Satiko, Patricia Washhington, Karin Dolores, Patchwork Passo a Passo, Tânia Mogari or Carolina Oneto (tracker pages exist but snippets lacked numbers; Social Blade could not be fetched).
- Fabiana Giandoso and "Ateliê Cheiro de Pano" returned no results at all (spelling or handle needed).
- Arte de Paninho's 845K figure is self-reported in her Instagram bio as captured by the search index; not confirmed by a tracker.
- Estela Mota's ~2.3K followers looks small for a Bernina ambassador; there may be another main account.
- No Brazilian creator was found with a public sponsor from Brother, Janome Brasil or fabric mills (Fernando Maluhy, Círculo etc.); a search for such partnerships returned nothing.
- No public business emails found except Natália Engel's; Ana Cosentino lists phone/address on her site.

## Biggest English-language quilting YouTubers, and whether they promote design software

### Takeaway
English-language channels are an order of magnitude bigger (Missouri Star 1M, Jordan Fabrics ~740K, Just Get It Done Quilts ~494K, Fat Quarter Shop ~425K) and many are shop-owned; design-software sponsorship is rare and small: the only paid case found is Electric Quilt sponsoring two Chatterbox Quilts videos (88.6K subs) in late 2025, plus PreQuilt's partnership with Suzy Quilts.

### Cited Findings
- Missouri Star Quilt Co. reached 1 million subscribers on 4 May 2026 (999,974 the day before, after a countdown from 30 Dec 2025) per its own blog — [Missouri Star blog](https://www.missouriquiltco.com/blogs/missouri-star-blog/the-longest-quilt-in-the-world); SponsorRadar lists 1,000,000 — [SponsorRadar](https://sponsorradar.com/channels/missouriquiltco); older figures 902K — [Networthspot](https://networthspot.com/missouri-star-quilt-company/net-worth/).

### Inferences
- Even in English, design software is not a common YouTube sponsor; a quilt design app with revenue share would be a new kind of offer for Brazilian creators rather than a crowded one.
- Mid-size English creators' sponsorship rate (~US$129–259 per video for a 130K channel) gives a reference point: a recurring subscription share could look generous next to one-off fees.
- Shop-owned giants (Missouri Star, Fat Quarter Shop, Jordan Fabrics) are more likely to want their own tools or cart integrations than an affiliate share.
