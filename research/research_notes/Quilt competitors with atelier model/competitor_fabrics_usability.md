# Quilt design competitors: fabric libraries, block libraries, block drawing, languages, ease of use (update, Nov 2026)

Method note: WebFetch was blocked by the network policy (prequilt.com failed with ENOTFOUND on the first try), so nothing below comes from a full page read. Every finding comes from WebSearch result text (titles and snippets), and the citations are the URLs those results showed. 50 WebSearch calls were used. Many results came from vendors (App Store listings, the company's own site or blog). These are marked "(vendor claim)". Several "best quilt software" ranking sites (wifitalents, gitnux, zipdo, worldmetrics, ddg.wcroc.umn.edu, and clone pages such as sandbox2.memberium.com) look like low-quality SEO content. They are used only where nothing better was found, and are marked "(low-reliability listicle)".

## 1. Fabric libraries: size, brands, solids vs prints, how current, Brazilian/Latin American brands

### Takeaway
EQ8 is still far ahead on built-in fabric: about 6,000–6,200 scanned manufacturer fabrics, plus paid "EQ Stash" packs of about 1,100–1,250 new printed fabrics several times a year, plus a yearly "Membership Palette" and Moda's free monthly collection. Quiltster is the only other tool with a library curated from manufacturer files (sent by SKU, with release dates). PreQuilt is mostly solids (Kona, Moda Bella, Art Gallery, Tilda, FreeSpirit…), and printed fabrics are mainly ones the user imports on a paid plan. The newer apps (Quiltler 3, QuiltFit, Swatchit, QuiltWall) rely on the user's own stash photos or on solid colour matching. No competitor found includes any Brazilian or Latin American fabric brand.

### Cited Findings
**EQ8 (Electric Quilt)**
- EQ8 comes with about 6,200 fabrics (one vendor page) or "over 6,000" (EQ support). The library is split into Colors, Manufacturer Basics and Themes — [EQ support](https://support.electricquilt.com/?p=2614); [Cotton Patch UK](https://cottonpatch.co.uk/eq8-electric-quilt-8-quilt-software)
- EQ describes EQ8 as designing quilts "using 6,000+ manufacturer fabrics and copyright-free blocks" — [EQ support](https://support.electricquilt.com/?p=1599)
- EQ Stash packs are paid downloads of the "newest fabrics on the market from top manufacturers", each with 1,000+ fabrics in categories such as florals and geometrics — [EQ online shop](https://electricquilt.com/online-shop/); [EQ Stash Online 2019 Download 02](https://electricquilt.com/online-shop/eq-stash-online-2019-download-02)
- Recent packs: Stash 2025-01 has "1,200+ brand new fabrics" (Riley Blake, Timeless Treasures, FreeSpirit). Another 2025 pack has 1,140+ (Andover, Michael Miller, Tilda, Robert Kaufman). One release is listed at 1,240+. Stash 2023-03 had 1,200+ (Riley Blake, Northcott, Timeless Treasures, Hoffman). Packs are numbered -01/-02/-03, which suggests about three releases a year — [EQ classes site](https://classes.electricquilt.com); [EQ support](https://support.electricquilt.com/?p=1403); [EQ Stash Online 2017 Download 03](https://electricquilt.com/online-shop/eq-stash-online-2017-download-03/)
- Brands named for Stash packs: Benartex, Moda, Tilda, Hoffman and others — [EQ support](https://support.electricquilt.com/?p=2614)
- Moda's own site says EQ8 users can "download a free collection each month, purchase fabric add-ons from EQ (that always include Moda's newest lines) or import images of fabrics in your own Stash". Moda also publishes fabric-swatch downloads per line, and EQ support notes Moda swatches are usually to scale — [Moda Fabrics](https://my.modafabrics.com/node/31813); [Chicken Scratch NY](https://chickenscratchny.com/adding-fabrics-to-electric-quilt-8/); [EQ support](https://support.electricquilt.com/?p=7925)
- There is also a separate yearly "EQ Membership Palette" collection: Fabric Library > EQ Membership Palettes > <YEAR> Membership — [EQ support: Membership Palette FAQ](https://support.electricquilt.com/articles/membership-palette-faq)
- EQ Mini (the lighter product) has 300 pieced and appliqué blocks and 3,500 scanned fabrics — [EQ shop: EQ Mini](https://electricquilt.com/online-shop/eq-mini)
- No EQ9 was found. EQ8 is still the flagship in 2026, and EQ's 2026 release is "Quilt Journeys" (state and home block add-on) — [Barbara Brackman blog, Jan 2026](https://barbarabrackman.blogspot.com/2026/01/quilt-journeys-state-blocks-for-eq.html)

- Colour swatches from "Kona, Art Gallery, Moda, Tilda and lots more" — [Stitched in Color](https://www.stitchedincolor.com/blog/2024/7/3/welcome-prequilt)

- A 2026 review gives Basic at $50/year and found the free demo could not save the test quilt — [Omid Saffari review](https://omidsaffari.com/blog/prequilt-review-for-fabric-color-planning)

- For scale, the main solid lines are: Kona about 366 colours (card 243, another blog says 303), Moda Bella about 210, Art Gallery Pure Solids 183 + 20 Signature = 203 — [Fat Quarter Shop blog](https://blog.fatquartershop.com/notion-of-month-color-cards/); [Stitched in Color substack](https://stitchedincolor.substack.com/p/comparing-quilting-cotton-solids); [Fat Quarter Shop](https://www.fatquartershop.com/pure-solids-color-card-supplement-art-gallery-fabrics); [Missouri Star listing](https://missouriquiltco.com/collections/kona-cotton-solids-fabric/products/kona-cotton-waterfall-palette-ten-squares)

**Quiltster**
- The library is built from manufacturer files: companies send 72 dpi JPG or PNG files with no logos or rulers, each named by exact SKU, in one folder per designer, with shop and consumer release dates. Without dates, collections stay visible only to subscribers. Swatch size sets the print scale in the quilt (vendor claim) — [Quiltster: Fabric Submission Guidelines](https://blog.quiltster.com/?p=10650)
- Quiltster says it "works closely with manufacturers and shop partners". Requests are ranked by popularity, availability and partner access, and there is a public request form (vendor claim) — [Quiltster: Request a Fabric Collection](https://blog.quiltster.com/?p=10404)
- In about 2021 it had "over 65 preloaded fabric collections" (old figure) — [Quiltworx](https://www.quiltworx.com/?p=83773)
- Users can upload their own fabric — [Quiltster: Upload Your Own Fabric](https://blog.quiltster.com/?p=3510)

**Quiltler 3 (iOS/iPadOS, Creative Greenlight LLC)**

**QuiltWall**
- Users photograph their stash, and the app auto-detects and names colours. It has 200+ blocks, core features are free with no sign-up, and the desktop version is Windows 10/11 (vendor claim) — [QuiltWall site](https://humannpc.pages.dev/quiltwall/); [QuiltWall block library](https://humannpc.pages.dev/quiltwall/features/block-library.html)

**Quiltography (iPad, $14.99)**

**CINCHQuilt, NiftyFifty, QuiltLab**

- NiftyFifty: none of the search results described a manufacturer fabric library. Its T-shirt quilt tool keeps shirt photos on the device (vendor claim) — [NiftyFifty](https://niftyfiftyquilting.com/t-shirt-quilt-designer)
- QuiltLab (quiltlab.co): described as a free browser studio with block editor, layout and "fabric library management". No brands were named — [wifitalents (low-reliability listicle)](https://wifitalents.com/best/quilt-block-design-software/)

**eQuilter Free Pattern Designer (a retailer, not a design app)**
- It customises eQuilter's own patterns "using our selection of over 20,000 fabrics" (current retail stock, prints included). Users drag fabric thumbnails from their Wish List onto the pattern, then "Add to Cart" — [eQuilter help](https://www.equilter.com/help/free_pattern_designer); [eQuilter pattern page](https://www.equilter.com/pattern/82/royal-fans)

**Brazilian / Latin American brands**

- Brazilian patchwork fabric brands sold on Magazine Luiza include Fabricart and Fabricart Signature (tricoline), Peripan (tricoline), Fernando Maluhy (themed patchwork prints), Estilotex (Estilotex Luxo patchwork prints) and Happydo — [Magalu: tricoline](https://www.magazineluiza.com.br/busca/tricoline); [Magalu: Fernando Maluhy](https://m.magazineluiza.com.br/busca/fernando+maluhy/); [Magalu: Estilotex](https://www.magazineluiza.com.br/busca/tecido+estilotex); [Magalu: Happydo](https://www.magazineluiza.com.br/busca/happydo+brasil+patch/)

### Inferences
- "Current collections" matters: only EQ (via paid Stash packs and Moda's monthly freebie) and Quiltster (manufacturer SKU feeds with release dates) keep up with new collections. The newer apps get around the licensing work by relying on users' own photos or on solids.
- A library with Brazilian brands (Fabricart, Peripan, Maluhy, Estilotex, Karsten Ateliê, Happydo) would be unique. No competitor touches it.
- Quiltster's submission rules (SKU-named 72 dpi files, release dates, swatch scale) are a ready-made template for asking manufacturers for files.
- To match EQ8 in raw count (6,000+ built in plus about 3,500 new a year), a large-scale manufacturer feed would be needed. Matching Quiltster-style curated collections is more realistic at launch.

### Gaps
- PreQuilt's exact manufacturer list and swatch count, and whether any printed manufacturer collections come built in (official pages were unreachable).
- The exact EQ Stash release cadence for 2026 and the price of the Membership Palette.
- Quiltster's current collection count and named manufacturer partners.
- Whether NiftyFifty, Quiltler or QuiltLab have any manufacturer-licensed swatches.

## 2. Block libraries and block drawing tools (and automatic cutting for drawn blocks)

### Takeaway
Block counts: EQ8 is the largest (thousands, plus the 4,000+ BlockBase+ add-on). NiftyFifty claims 1,200–1,400+ real swap blocks (its own pages disagree). QuiltWall has 200+, PreQuilt 100+, EQ Mini 300. Quiltler 3, QuiltFit and Swatchit have only a few classic blocks. Drawn blocks get automatic cutting instructions only in EQ8 (Rotary Cutting Chart, with exceptions such as kites, plus templates) and NiftyFifty's Pattern Designer (described as turning a drawn block into a "sewable pattern" with cutting charts and templates). PreQuilt has a block editor and printable pattern pages.

### Cited Findings
- EQ8: drawing with EasyDraw "similar to drawing with pencil and paper". All segments must connect to each other and to the block outline. Snap settings and these rules are the usual stumbling block, and EQ advises "a lot of time experimenting" first — [EQ support: Understanding EasyDraw](https://support.electricquilt.com/articles/understanding-easydraw-in-eq8); [EQ classes: Drawing Pieced Blocks in EQ8](https://classes.electricquilt.com/class-catalog/drawing-pieced-blocks-in-eq8)
- EQ8 Rotary Cutting Chart: the user enters any finished block size, seam allowance 0.125–1 in is added automatically, and rounded or unrounded values can be previewed. "Not all patches are rotary cuttable": some (for example 30° and 90° kites) are left out and must be printed as templates — [EQ support: Not All Patches are Rotary Cuttable](https://support.electricquilt.com/?p=3773); [EQ support](https://support.electricquilt.com/?p=2837)
- An Amazon reviewer said EQ8 gives no instructions for assembling the top and no binding in its estimates — [Fakespot page](https://www.fakespot.com/product/electric-quilt-a-0008eq)
- EQ's BlockBase+ add-on has 4,000+ pieced blocks, exportable as JPG/PNG/SVG — [EQ support: BlockBase+](https://support.electricquilt.com/products/blockbaseplus)
- NiftyFifty Quilt Designer: arrange "1,400+ real handmade blocks on grids up to 20×20". Another NiftyFifty page says "more than 1,200 handmade blocks collected through block swaps since 1997" (conflict, both vendor claims) — [NiftyFifty Quilt Designer](https://niftyfiftyquilting.com/quilt-designer); [NiftyFifty](https://niftyfiftyquilting.com/)
- NiftyFifty Pattern Designer: page title "Free Quilt Block Design Tool — Cutting Charts & Templates". It "turns your on-screen design into a real, sewable pattern", runs in the browser, and needs no download or account (vendor claim) — [NiftyFifty Pattern Designer](https://niftyfiftyquilting.com/pattern-designer)
- NiftyFifty Modern Quilt Designer: negative-space and irregular grids. "Print Pattern" gives a cutting chart and numbered assembly steps. NiftyFifty also opens EQ8 .pj8 and EQ7 .pj7 files free in the browser (vendor claim) — [NiftyFifty Modern](https://niftyfiftyquilting.com/modern-quilt-designer); [NiftyFifty EQ8 alternatives](https://niftyfiftyquilting.com/quilt-design-software/eq8-alternatives)
- An independent comparison found the NiftyFifty Pattern Designer saves only in the browser, and advises downloading a file — [QuiltLab blog](https://www.quiltlab.co/blog/free-quilt-design-software)
- PreQuilt: 100+ blocks, a block editor ("Design your own blocks using our block editor"), mirror blocks and "Magic Quad" tutorials, and a colour randomizer. The layout becomes printable pattern pages with seam allowances (the last point is from a listicle) — [Canadian Quilter](https://canadianquilter.com/?p=41598); [Stitched in Color tips](https://stitchedincolor.com/blog/2024/7/22/5-tips-to-get-started-with-prequilt); [zipdo (low-reliability listicle)](https://zipdo.co/best/quilt-design-software/)
- PreQuilt's yardage calculator works only for quilts built in PreQuilt, not for designers' coloring pages — [Omid Saffari review](https://omidsaffari.com/blog/prequilt-review-for-fabric-color-planning)
- Quiltler 3: classic blocks (log cabin, HST, flying geese) and per-fabric yardage from the actual design. No written cutting instructions for a custom block were found (vendor claim) — [App Store: Quiltler 3](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6755831082)
- QuiltFit: classic blocks (Nine Patch, Log Cabin, HST, Flying Geese), set by width and height in blocks and finished block size. Pro (subscription, 3-day trial) unlocks Magic Fill, cutting layouts and PDF export. It also has a shopping list with quantities and optional prices — [App Store: QuiltFit](https://apps.apple.com/pl/app/id6776541705)
- Quiltography: claims "more block designs than any other mobile app", each named, plus a template block designer (vendor claim). A 2013 forum user couldn't change blocks — [App Store: Quiltography](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quiltography-quilt-design-made-simple/id585991997); [Quiltingboard](https://www.quiltingboard.com/main-f1/redwork-question-t225690-next.html)
- QuiltWall: 200+ traditional and modern blocks and a "Block Studio" (vendor claim) — [QuiltWall block library](https://humannpc.pages.dev/quiltwall/features/block-library.html); [QuiltWall Block Studio](https://humannpc.pages.dev/quiltwall/features/block-studio.html)
- QuiltLab: block editor that supports foundation paper piecing and traditional piecing, focused on block design and reference printing — [wifitalents (low-reliability listicle)](https://wifitalents.com/best/quilt-block-design-software/)
- For foundation paper piecing, an r/FPP user said Quilt Assistant is much easier than EQ8 and uses EQ8 only for fabric mock-ups — [r/FPP thread (mirror)](https://reddit.birdcat.cafe/r/FPP/comments/1j0kx2l/how_to_start_designing_patterns)

### Inferences
- The owner's drawing tool (grid-paper and cells modes, seam lines, symmetry, repeat preview, automatic cutting list in in/cm) directly matches EQ8's EasyDraw + Rotary Cutting Chart and NiftyFifty's Pattern Designer. The pitch is "EQ8's drawing power without EQ8's rules". EQ8's connectivity and snap rules are the documented pain point.
- Cm cutting lists are a real gap: every competitor found works in inches by default. No metric cutting output was confirmed for any of them (not verified either way).
- NiftyFifty is the closest free web rival on blocks + drawing + cutting charts. Its self-reported counts are inconsistent, so "1,400+" should be cited as a vendor claim.

### Gaps
- Whether EQ8 PatchDraw (curved and appliqué) shapes get rotary charts (likely templates only). This was not confirmed.
- How NiftyFifty's Pattern Designer handles non-rotary shapes. Its languages and units were not found.
- PreQuilt's exact block count in 2026, and whether its block editor gives cutting instructions for custom blocks.

## 3. Ease of use, onboarding, accessibility for older users

### Takeaway
EQ8 is consistently described as powerful with a real learning curve ("huge learning curve" for some, "not brain surgery" for others). A whole ecosystem of books (EQ8 Lessons for Beginners), paid classes and YouTube exists to get people over it. PreQuilt is described as "easy once you get the hang of it", with a free YouTube course and help guides. No competitor markets large text or senior-friendly screens. The only "beginner computer user" product, EQ's Quilt Design Wizard, has been discontinued.

### Cited Findings
- EQ8 forum views: "huge learning curve", with time to learn as the main obstacle. Others say it is learnable ("isn't brain surgery"; "you do have to invest some time"). YouTube tutorials help. Mostly 2019-era posts — [Quiltingboard: To EQ8 or not?](https://www.quiltingboard.com/main-f1/eq8-not-t306667-2.html); [Quiltingboard](https://www.quiltingboard.com/main-f1/printfriendly-eq8-not-t306667-2.html)
- EQ8 was seen as easier than EQ7 ("could not believe how much easier EQ8 is"). A retailer says the redesign has "large interface elements for faster, more intuitive learning" (retailer claim) — [Quiltingboard](https://www.quiltingboard.com/main-f1/opinions-latest-software-t295639.html); [HC Quilts](https://hcquilts.com/products/software-electric-quilt-8-quilt-design-eq8)
- EQ8 onboarding is largely paid or external: the "EQ8 Lessons for Beginners" book is widely sold and recommended, there are EQ online classes (Drawing Pieced Blocks, Designing Your Own Blocks), and AQS teaches "Design your first quilt in EQ8" workshops — [Searchpress: EQ8 Lessons for Beginners](https://searchpress.com/book/9781893824928/eq8-lessons-for-beginners); [EQ classes](https://classes.electricquilt.com/class-catalog/designing-your-own-blocks); [American Quilter](https://preview.americanquilter.com/design-your-first-quilt-in-eq8)
- A blogger: drawing your own EQ8 blocks "will take time and patience" (block size and drawing settings must be set yourself) — [UKQU](https://ukqu.co.uk/?p=302845)
- PreQuilt: "easy to use once you get the hang of it". The developers acknowledge a learning curve and wrote help guides. There is a free "Introduction to PreQuilt" course on YouTube plus screenshot guides. Stitched in Color recommends starting with the free demo — [sewingmachinefun](https://www.sewingmachinefun.com/free-quilt-design-software/); [Stitched in Color tips](https://www.stitchedincolor.com/blog/2024/7/22/5-tips-to-get-started-with-prequilt); [Canadian Quilter](https://canadianquilter.com/members-only-studio/visualize-your-next-quilt-with-prequilt)
- PreQuilt is best as a colour and print-scale explorer. EQ8 is recommended for "depth for original designs" — [Omid Saffari review (ES)](https://omidsaffari.com/es/blog/programa-de-diseno-de-patchwork-prequilt)
- Quilt Wizard (iOS) reviewers: "controls are a little clunky and unintuitive"; "the interface is not remotely intuitive" — [AppFollow: Quilt Wizard](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/quilt-wizard/475622761?country=us)
- EQ's Quilt Design Wizard was made "for beginning quilters and beginning computer users", with step-by-step point-and-click, and is no longer sold — [EQ shop: Quilt Design Wizard](https://electricquilt.com/online-shop/quilt-design-wizard)
- No quilt design software marketed with large text or accessibility features for older users was found — [search results incl. Quilting Daily](https://www.quiltingdaily.com/workshop-wednesday-easy-quilt-design-software-mccalls-quilting-blog)
- Newer apps' reviews are thin: Swatchit and QuiltFit listings had too few ratings to show an overview — [App Store: Swatchit](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6760553382); [App Store: QuiltFit](https://apps.apple.com/pl/app/id6776541705)
- No independent reviews of QuiltWall were found — [QuiltWall site](https://humannpc.pages.dev/quiltwall/)

### Inferences
- "Ease of use for a 67-year-old quilter" is an open position. EQ8's learning is outsourced to books and classes, PreQuilt is "easy once you get it", and nobody advertises large text, simple modes or guided tours. The owner's guided tour and large-text design would be a real differentiator, but would need user testing to back the claim.
- EQ8's teacher ecosystem (books, AQS workshops, EQ classes) is also a moat. A teacher or "atelier" channel in Portuguese and Spanish could copy it where EQ has none.

### Gaps
- No recent (2025–2026) independent usability studies, ratings or Reddit threads comparing EQ8, PreQuilt, NiftyFifty and Quiltler. Results were mostly 2019–2024 forum posts.
- No information on in-app guided tours or onboarding inside NiftyFifty, Quiltler 3 or QuiltFit.

## 5. Linking fabrics to buying (shop links, kits, carts)

### Takeaway
Two models exist. First, a retailer-owned designer: eQuilter's Free Pattern Designer, with 20,000+ in-stock fabrics, drag-onto-pattern and Add to Cart. Second, a marketplace: Quiltster, where partner shops (Quiltworx, Always Quilting, quilt shops) sell kits and fabric, each listing labelled "Sold by", with in-app yardage and kit purchase. QuiltFit makes a shopping list, Quiltler 3 "pairs with" Missouri Star and Kaufman, and EQ8, PreQuilt and NiftyFifty show no automatic cart filling. ### Cited Findings
- eQuilter: customise their patterns with any of 20,000+ fabrics. Fabrics placed in the Wish List appear in a Fabric Selector, the user drags thumbnails onto the pattern, then clicks Add to Cart (the pattern "as configured" can be ordered) — [eQuilter help](https://www.equilter.com/help/free_pattern_designer); [eQuilter pattern: Royal Fans](https://www.equilter.com/pattern/82/royal-fans)

No subscription is needed to shop, but the Quilt Planner (trying real fabrics before buying) needs one. Each listing shows "Sold by", and kits can be bought in the app with yardage provided (vendor claim) — [Quiltster: Who Fulfills Marketplace Orders?](https://blog.quiltster.com/?p=9996); [Quiltster: What is Quiltster?](https://blog.quiltster.com/?p=11071); [Quiltster: Our Partners](https://blog.quiltster.com/?p=11388)

### Inferences
- Filling carts automatically across several brands and shops (international and Brazilian) would go beyond both existing models. eQuilter is limited to one shop and its own patterns, and Quiltster to partner-shop kits.
- Quiltster's "Sold by" marketplace model and its manufacturer pitch (kit marketing, inventory) are the closest template for revenue sharing with shops.
