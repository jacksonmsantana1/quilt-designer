# Teacher channel, revenue share, pricing and payments for a paid quilt design app in Brazil / LatAm (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: web page fetching was blocked in this environment (a single WebFetch to asaas.com failed with "host not allowed"), so every finding below comes from web-search result text and the URLs the search showed. 54 searches were used. Many fee figures come from third-party comparison blogs, not the providers' own pricing pages; this is flagged item by item. Treat fee numbers as "check before you build" values.

## 1. Do quilt software competitors have teacher / educator / school programs or revenue shares?

### Takeaway
None of the quilt design tools found pays teachers a recurring cut of student subscriptions. Electric Quilt (EQ) is the closest: it supports independent teachers with a credentials form, teacher-only resources, class listings and a 14-day trial for classrooms, but it offers no certification and no published commission. Revenue shares in the quilting world are for content (Creative Spark 50/50 on video classes; Craftsy's royalties, later cut), and the sewing-machine brands run ambassador programs that pay in loaned machines and discounts, not cash. A teacher revenue share would make this app stand out from every quilt-software competitor found.

### Cited Findings
**Electric Quilt (EQ8)**
- EQ ran a full-day seminar, "How to Teach an EQ8 Class" (lesson plans, classroom setup, learning styles). It also covers "how to request and use the 14-day trial from EQ for classroom situations." — [EQ class catalog, S2019](https://classes.electricquilt.com/class-catalog/how-to-teach-an-eq8-class/); [EQ class overview](https://classes.electricquilt.com/class-catalog/how-to-teach-an-eq8-class-2)
- "The Electric Quilt Company does not offer teacher certification." Completing the class can be listed as experience on a résumé. — [EQ, How to Teach an EQ8 Class](https://classes.electricquilt.com/class-catalog/how-to-teach-an-eq8-class/)
- Independent teachers who run online EQ classes fill in an "EQ Teacher Credentials form to gain access to our teacher-only resources and list your classes on our website." EQ also lists "teachers for hire" pages (e.g. "willing to travel"). — [EQ independent online classes](https://classes.electricquilt.com/home/independent-online-eq-classes); [EQ teachers for hire, Elenore Busby](https://classes.electricquilt.com/teachersforhire/elenore-busby)
- Students in teacher-led classes must bring a laptop "with Electric Quilt 8 (EQ8) installed and validated", so each student buys their own licence. — [EQ8 beginner class](https://classes.electricquilt.com/class-catalog/eq8-beginner-class)
- EQ also sells its own self-paced video classes ("EQ At Home"), with lifetime access for the life of EQ8, $265 if bundled. EQ is thus partly a competitor to the teachers it supports. — [What is EQ At Home?](https://classes.electricquilt.com/home/eq-at-home-faq)
- EQ8 lists at $239.95 (one-off licence). Shops resell it through a wholesale account application (sales@electricquilt.com). The search found no published dealer margin. — [EQ online shop](https://electricquilt.com/online-shop/category/electric-quilt-8-eq8); [Become an EQ retailer](https://electricquilt.com/retailers/become-an-eq-retailer/)
- An affiliate directory lists Electric Quilt as a merchant on networks such as ShareASale, with no rate shown. The search found no official EQ affiliate terms. — [affsignal.com](https://affsignal.com/merchant/Electricquilt)

**PreQuilt, Quiltler, mySewnet**
- PreQuilt has two subscription types, "basic and market". The Market plan is for pattern designers who sell their work using PreQuilt. A Sept 2026 review lists Market at $10/month or $100/year. The search found no sign that PreQuilt pays designers or teachers a revenue share: designers pay PreQuilt, not the other way around. — [While She Naps](https://whileshenaps.com/prequilt-is-a-sleek-new-design-tool-for-quilters/); [omidsaffari.com PreQuilt review](https://omidsaffari.com/blog/prequilt-review-for-fabric-color-planning)
- Quiltler: third-party reviews put a paid plan at $7.99/month or $49.99/year and a "Designer" tier at $99/year. The free plan allows one saved quilt and five stored fabrics. The sources are inconsistent and the search found no teacher or affiliate programme. The iOS listing shows $9.99. — [omidsaffari.com](https://omidsaffari.com/blog/prequilt-review-for-fabric-color-planning); [App Store, Quiltler](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1120958195)
- mySewnet (embroidery software from the Husqvarna Viking/Pfaff family) is subscription-only: Silver about $9.99/month, Gold $19.99/month, Platinum $39.99/month. Dealers can sell a prepaid 12-month code "for the cost of 10 months". This is a dealer channel, not a teacher channel. — [mySewnet plans (US)](https://account.mysewnet.com/en-us/products-and-plans); [Cole's Sewing Centre](https://www.colessewingcentre.co.uk/620155521-169083.html)

**Machine brand ambassadors (Bernina, Janome, Brother)**
- The BERNINA/bernette Ambassador Class of 2026–27 gets a loaned machine, a retreat, quarterly webinars, reduced prices on products and a possible "Loyalist" path afterwards. It requires machine exclusivity. The 2024 call described an 18-month term, Learning Center access, dealer support and a mentor, with the Artisan/Educator track limited to US residents. — [WeAllSew, Class of 2026 open call](https://weallsew.com/?p=129546); [WeAllSew, Class of 2024](https://weallsew.com/?p=117250)
- A former ambassador says the machine is only on loan "for the duration of my ambassadorship". — [christaquilts.com](https://christaquilts.com/2015/12/01/bernina-love-and-what-it-means-to-be-a-brand-ambassador/)
- Janome has "Janome Educators" profile pages, and at least some educators are staff or ex-staff. The search found no public benefit package and no Brother educator programme. — [Janome Educators](https://www.janome.com/janome-educators)

**Craft video platforms (Craftsy/Bluprint, Creative Spark / C&T Publishing)**
- Craftsy/Bluprint moved instructors from a share of each class sold to pay based on "minutes watched". All twelve instructors interviewed reported "a similarly dramatic drop in income". The amended contracts also added a "first look" clause. — [Craft Industry Alliance](https://craftindustryalliance.org/tag/rebranding/)
- One former Craftsy instructor said his royalty check had fallen to about $60. — [Craft Industry Alliance via search](https://craftindustryalliance.org/?p=9423)
- Creative Spark (C&T): "standard revenue share is 50/50 with all processing fees and site fees being covered by Creative Spark". An older page mentions 70/30 when Creative Spark does the filming, without saying who gets 70%. Instructors usually film and edit themselves. — [C&T Become an Instructor](https://ctpub.com/pages/become-an-instructor); [Creative Spark instructor info 2026](https://ctpub.com/pages/creative-spark-instructor-info)

### Inferences
- The model "the teacher earns from each student's software subscription" seems to be unclaimed in quilting software. EQ's programme shows that teachers are already the way quilters learn design software, but EQ pays them nothing and sells competing video classes.
- The Craftsy story is the main warning for teachers' trust in the quilting world: a platform cut teacher pay one-sidedly after a model change. Teachers who know that story will want written, stable terms (rate, duration, payout schedule) and a way to see their own numbers.
- EQ's "14-day trial for classrooms" is the friction this app can remove: a browser app has nothing to install or validate, and a teacher account can be free.

### Gaps
- The search found no EQ dealer margin, no EQ affiliate rate, no PreQuilt affiliate terms and no Quiltler teacher plan. These would need the vendors' own pages, which could not be fetched.
- No Brazilian or LatAm quilt software with a teacher programme turned up. Hotmart course sellers are the nearest local "teacher monetisation" pattern (section 2).

## 2. Precedents for "free for teachers, paid by students, teacher gets a cut", and typical percentages and pitfalls

### Takeaway
Brazil already has a widely understood pattern for paying teachers a share: Hotmart, Kiwify and Eduzz "afiliado" and "coprodutor" commissions, where the producer sets the rate. Affiliate rates for digital courses run high (often 30–70%) because the course is pure margin. SaaS affiliate norms are lower: about 20–30% recurring, often capped at 12–24 months. "Free for teachers" programmes such as Canva for Education and Duolingo for Schools are used to grow the user base, not to pay teachers cash. The main pitfalls are commissions that outlast the student's value (churn after the course), trust problems from poor tracking, and teachers whose classes the platform could compete with.

### Cited Findings
**Brazilian infoproduct platforms**
- Hotmart: the affiliate commission is taken from the net amount, then the co-producer commission is calculated on what is left. The official example uses 35% for the affiliate and 10% for the co-producer, and the producer may set a different rate for affiliate-generated sales. — [Hotmart help: como meu faturamento é calculado](https://help.hotmart.com/pt-br/article/360011780352/como-meu-faturamento-e-calculado-na-hotmart-); [Hotmart help: coprodução](https://help.hotmart.com/pt-br/article/215828398/como-configurar-a-coproducao-de-um-produto-)
- Hotmart affiliate commission is "calculated on the sale value after Hotmart's service fee". Attribution follows priority rules (link, cookie, coupon, purchase history). — [Hotmart help: critérios de priorização](https://help.hotmart.com/pt-br/article/36806399753485/quais-sao-os-criterios-de-priorizacao-na-atribuicao-de-comissao-para-afiliados-na-hotmart-)
- Hotmart's platform fee is reported as 9.9% + R$1 per sale (20% for products of R$10 or less). One blog says the fixed part rises to R$2.49 from 21 Sept 2026, which is unconfirmed. — [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/mercado/o-que-e-e-como-funciona-a-hotmart/); [Tactus](https://tactus.com.br/taxas-da-hotmart-para-produtor/)
- Typical affiliate rates on Hotmart are reported very differently: "40% to 70% or more" in one blog and "5% to 20%" in another, and "up to 70%" in a marketing piece. — [Hostinger BR](https://www.hostinger.com/br/tutoriais/como-vender-na-hotmart-como-afiliado); [Hostgator BR](https://www.hostgator.com.br/blog/vender-afiliado-hotmart/)
- Hotmart subscriptions: the producer sets currency, price per cycle and period (weekly to annual). Up to five automatic retry attempts per cycle. — [Hotmart help: como funciona um plano de assinatura](https://help.hotmart.com/pt-br/article/208424858/como-funciona-um-plano-de-assinatura-)
- Kiwify (third-party sources): 8.99% + R$2.49 per sale, no monthly fee, unlimited co-producers per product with automatic split. Affiliate commission is set by the producer at 1%–80%. A product needs R$5,000 in sales before it can be opened to affiliates. Subscriptions are supported by card, Pix and boleto. — [Mobills](https://www.mobills.com.br/blog/ganhar-dinheiro/kiwify-afiliados/); [Reportei](https://reportei.com/kiwify-o-que-e/)
- Kiwify also has a separate producer-referral programme: 1.5% of the referred account's net revenue for 12 months. — [quasa.io](https://quasa.io/pt/media/hotmart-ou-kiwify-afiliados-e-prazo-pesam-mais-que-a-taxa)
- Eduzz (third-party sources): 4.9% + R$2.49 per direct sale, and 8.9% + R$1.00 (or R$2.49, sources conflict) via an affiliate. There is no published co-production fee: the split is agreed between the partners. — [Tactus](https://tactus.com.br/?p=49084); [bit4learn](https://bit4learn.com/pt/?p=1434)
- Patchwork courses already sell on Hotmart (e.g. "Curso básico de Patchwork", Graça Freitas; a Spanish beginners' patchwork course). A sewing "CLUB" (Método Inaá) is sold as an auto-renewing annual Hotmart subscription. — [Hotmart: Curso básico de Patchwork](https://hotmart.com/pt-br/marketplace/produtos/curso-basico-de-patchwork/W77194113F); [Hotmart ES: Aprendamos Patchwork](https://hotmart.com:443/es/marketplace/productos/aprendamos-patchwork-juntas-modulo1/G53440466D); [Inaá terms PDF](https://comunidadeinaa.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Termos-e-Condicoes-11.04.2024.pdf)

**SaaS affiliate and referral norms**
- Rewardful: most SaaS programmes pay 20–30% of revenue, up to 40% for top affiliates, with an average of 24.16%. LinkJolt data (121 campaigns) gives a median of 20% and an average of 22.9%. Recurring payouts are often limited to 12–24 months. 71% of SaaS affiliate programmes pay recurring commissions. — [Track360 2026 benchmark](https://www.track360.io/blog/saas-affiliate-commission-rates-benchmark-2026); [Rewardful](https://www.rewardful.com/articles/affiliate-commission-explained); [Refgrow 2026](https://refgrow.com/blog/affiliate-program-benchmarks-2026)
- Tiered example: 15% recurring for 1–10 customers, rising to 25% for 26 or more. — [PromoteKit](https://www.promotekit.com/blog/how-to-set-affiliate-commission-rates)
- Tools: Rewardful and FirstPromoter both start at about $49/month, with tiers by affiliate revenue (FirstPromoter $49 up to $5k/month, $99 up to $15k; Rewardful $49 up to $7.5k). Both are built around Stripe. — [Affiverse](https://www.affiversemedia.com/best-affiliate-marketing-software-for-saas/); [G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/firstpromoter-vs-rewardful)
- Music-learning example: Skoove pays a 25% referral commission. JoyTunes gave registered teachers free access. Studio software such as My Music Staff charges teachers a flat fee (from £9.95/month) instead of sharing revenue. — [hypebot](https://www.hypebot.com/joytunes-raises-5-million-gives-registered-teachers-free-access); [Music Teacher Magazine](https://musicteachermagazine.co.uk/content/other/my-music-staff-app)

**"Free for teachers" programmes**
- Canva for Education is "100% free" for verified K-12 teachers and their students, with all Pro features. Students use it through their teacher's class. One guide says it reaches over 100 million students and teachers in 800,000 schools. — [Canva UK education](https://www.canva.com/en_gb/education/teachers/); [Style Factory 2026](https://www.stylefactoryproductions.com/blog/canva-for-education)
- Duolingo for Schools "was completely free for students and teachers. It wasn't a revenue stream", but it drove usage. — [Curious PM](https://curiouspm.substack.com/p/duolingo-the-backstory)

**Studio and gym aggregator precedent (Brazil)**
- Wellhub (ex-Gympass) pays partner gyms per check-in, monthly by default. In Aug 2026 it added a weekly payout option for a 1.49% fee. Neighbourhood gyms have been leaving because the payment per visit is a fraction of a direct monthly fee. One complaint cites R$28.93 per check-in. — [FM2S](https://www.fm2s.com.br/blog/gympass-wellhub); [Correio 24 Horas](https://www.correio24horas.com.br/salvador/fim-do-gympass-academias-de-bairro-rompem-com-plataformas-e-tendencia-cresce-no-pais-0226); [Reclame Aqui](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/wellhub/valores-de-repasse_6vwjURMH8GWoRWNB/)

**Pitfalls**
- Unreliable tracking makes affiliates lose trust, and a poor affiliate experience makes them disengage. — [Refgrow: SaaS affiliate mistakes](https://refgrow.com/blog/saas-affiliate-program-mistakes)
- Recurring commissions can erode net revenue retention when affiliates bring accounts that churn before the commission cost is recovered. Rank by expected lifetime payout = (price × rate) ÷ monthly churn. — [Refgrow](https://refgrow.com/blog/saas-affiliate-program-mistakes); [StackSkeptic](https://t.me/s/StackSkeptic/15)
- In education, the person who recommends is often not the person who pays, so rewards should match what the recommender values. — [Referral Rock: education referral programs](https://referralrock.com/blog/education-referral-programs/)
- Some programmes owe commission only on payments actually received: none for a period the client cancels, downgrades or doesn't pay. — [Univers Studio affiliate terms](https://univers.studio/pt/affiliates/terms/)

### Inferences
- A teacher share of about 20–30% of each student payment, for as long as the student keeps paying, matches SaaS norms. It is generous compared with Kiwify's 1.5% producer referral, but well below Hotmart course affiliate rates. Brazilian teachers who know Hotmart may compare it with "40–70%". Framing it as "income that keeps coming in for every student who stays" rather than as a percentage will probably land better.
- To limit the "course ended, student cancelled" problem: pay the teacher only on payments actually received (no upfront bounty), and add features that keep students after the course (saving their own designs, the fabric library, PDF templates). Consider a lifetime attribution so a student who comes back months later still credits the teacher.
- Channel conflict: if the app also sells directly (ads, app stores), decide up front whether a student who signs up alone and later joins a teacher's class gets re-attributed. Hotmart's priority rules (link → cookie → coupon → history) are a model. A per-teacher coupon code is the easiest thing to explain in a class.
- Wellhub shows that small studios leave platforms when their share feels small next to their own fees. Since atelier classes cost about R$150/month (section 5), a few reais per student is a small bonus, not income. The pitch should stress free software for the teacher, better classes and less admin, with the commission as a bonus.

### Gaps
- The search could not confirm whether Hotmart or Kiwify affiliates earn on every subscription renewal or only on the first charge; official help pages could not be fetched.
- No measured data on churn after a course ends, or on Canva/Duolingo education-to-paid conversion.
- No specific Brazilian music-school or language-school revenue-share figures were found.

## 3. Brazilian payment infrastructure for subscriptions with automatic split to teachers, plus fees, tax and legal points

### Takeaway
Several Brazilian providers can run a monthly subscription and split each payment automatically to a teacher's account: Asaas (subaccounts + split on subscriptions), Pagar.me (recurrence + split recipients), Mercado Pago (marketplace split, 1:1 standard), Iugu and Vindi. Stripe Connect supports Pix and, since April 2026, recurring Pix. Pix Automático (live since 16 June 2025) gives card-free automatic monthly debits and is reported to raise subscription retention a lot (Hotmart +32 points). Card fees run about 3–5%. Pix is cheaper (Asaas R$1.99 flat; Iugu 0.99%). On taxes, each sale needs an NFS-e with ISS (2–5% by city), the 2026 IBS/CBS test fields, and a decision on how teacher payouts are documented (a teacher with an MEI issues an NFS-e, one without an MEI is paid by RPA with withholdings).

### Cited Findings
**Pix Automático**
- The Banco Central set the launch for 16 June 2025 (Resolução BCB nº 402). It is free for the payer and meant for recurring bills including "assinaturas". The BC projected it would help 60 million people without credit cards. — [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/mercado/bc-confirma-lancamento-do-pix-automatico-em-junho-de-2025-296277/); [Times Brasil](https://timesbrasil.com.br/brasil/economia-brasileira/banco-central-lanca-hoje-o-pix-automatico-entenda-como-funciona/); [InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/minhas-financas/bc-faz-ajustes-em-seguranca-e-estabelece-junho-de-2025-para-lancar-pix-automatico/)
- Adoption: 9.5 million transactions and R$3.5 billion in Q2 2026, more than double Q1 in count. Receivers grew from about 1,000 at end-2025 to 14,000 by August 2026. May 2026 saw about 2.87 million transactions. Adyen reported barriers in authorisations and billing cycles. — [Finsiders Brasil](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/giro-noticias/pix-automatico-cresce-no-1o-semestre-de-2026-diz-bc/)
- Over 60% of Pix Automático users are new subscribers to digital platforms (EBANX, June 2026). — [FF News](https://ffnews.com/newsarticle/paytech/more-than-60-of-pix-automatico-users-are-brand-new-subscribers-to-digital-platforms-ebanx-data-shows/)
- Hotmart saw a 32 percentage point rise in retention for recurring Pix payments after adding Pix Automático via EBANX, converting more than four times as many previously failed Pix renewals. These are company-reported figures with no baseline given. — [EBANX press release](https://business.ebanx.com/en/press-room/press-releases/hotmart-sees-a-32-percentage-point-boost-in-retention-after-launching-recurring-payments-with-pix-via-ebanx)
- Pix share of online purchases: 42% in 2025 vs 41% for credit cards (EBANX). Projected 45% by end-2026 and 50% by 2028. Credit cards still lead by value, and installment card use is still the most-used method in one survey (53% vs 22%). — [Finsiders](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/estudos-e-relatorios/pix-deve-concentrar-metade-das-compras-online-no-brasil-ate-2028-projeta-ebanx/); [E-Commerce Brasil](https://www.ecommercebrasil.com.br/noticias/pix-ou-cartao-dados-mostram-como-o-brasileiro-paga-no-comercio-eletronico)

**Asaas**
- Subscriptions by boleto, Pix or card. Card renewals charge automatically. With a plain "subscription", Pix creates a new charge each cycle for the customer to pay. Pix Automático is a separate feature that works from a payer authorisation. — [Asaas FAQ assinaturas](https://docs.asaas.com/docs/faq-assinaturas); [Asaas: diferença entre Pix Automático e Assinaturas](https://docs.asaas.com/docs/diferen%C3%A7a-entre-pix-autom%C3%A1tico-e-assinaturas); [Asaas API Pix Automático](https://docs.asaas.com/reference/pix-automatico)
- Split works on subscription charges. If the split total is more than the net amount, the subscription is blocked temporarily and a webhook is sent. — [Asaas FAQ assinaturas](https://docs.asaas.com/docs/faq-assinaturas)
- How split works: each receiver has a walletId (returned when you create a subaccount by API). Split is fixed or percentage of the net value (after fees), and percentages cannot exceed 100%. Refunds reverse the split. Splits above the net amount are blocked with 2 business days to fix. The PAYMENT_SPLIT_DONE webhook fires on settlement. — [Asaas split docs](https://docs.asaas.com/docs/split-de-pagamentos); [Asaas payment split (EN)](https://docs.asaas.com/docs/payment-split)
- Fees (third-party comparison, checked 16 Oct 2026): R$1.99 per Pix or boleto received, card R$0.49 + 2.99%, no monthly fee. A 3-month intro offer was R$0.99 per Pix/boleto and card R$0.49 + 1.99%. — [quasa.io](https://quasa.io/pt/media/asaas-ou-iugu-tarifa-publicada-vence-ate-o-volume-pedir-proposta)
- An old WooCommerce plugin page said subscriptions were not compatible with split, which contradicts the current FAQ. — [WordPress plugin woo-asaas](https://br.wordpress.org/plugins/woo-asaas/)

**Mercado Pago**
- Subscriptions: no monthly fee. The subscriptions page lists card fees of 4.99% (immediate payout). The blog gives 4.99% immediate, 4.49% at 14 days and 3.99% at 30 days. Failed charges are retried automatically. Recurring charges don't block the card's full limit. — [Mercado Pago Assinaturas](https://www.mercadopago.com.br/ferramentas-para-vender/assinaturas); [MP blog](https://www.mercadopago.com.br/blog/como-receber-pagamentos-recorrentes)
- Split: model 1:1 is the standard (one seller per payment). 1:N is only for "carteira assessorada" sellers working with MP's sales team. Sellers need KYC level 6 and OAuth connection. The marketplace fee is set with `marketplace_fee` / `application_fee`. In 1:1 the marketplace can't refund in full if the seller lacks balance. — [MP split prerequisites](https://www.mercadopago.com.br/developers/pt/docs/split-payments/split-1-1/integration-configuration/integrate-marketplace.md); [MP prerequisites](https://mercadopago.com.br/developers/en/docs/split-payments/prerequisites)
- The search found no MP source combining subscriptions with split. MP also runs subscriptions in Mexico and Argentina (Subscription Plans, Subscriptions API). — [MP subscriptions overview](https://www.mercadopago.com.br/developers/en/docs/subscriptions/overview); [MP Mexico](https://www.mercadopago.com.mx/developers/en/docs/subscription-plans/overview)

**Pagar.me (Stone), Iugu, Vindi**
- Pagar.me supports subscriptions with split to one or more recipients (Stone marketing content, not technical documentation). The search found no current fee table. — [Stone: split com recorrência](https://conteudo.stone.com.br/split-de-pagamento-com-recorrencia/)
- Iugu "Essencial" plan (cited in a comparison): Pix 0.99%, boleto R$2.19, card 3.34%, plus R$0.40 processing per boleto or card. Plans include a monthly fee and possible setup fees. Iugu manages the Pix Automático subscription cycle (authorisations, scheduling, retries, cancellations). — [quasa.io](https://quasa.io/pt/media/asaas-ou-iugu-tarifa-publicada-vence-ate-o-volume-pedir-proposta); [Iugu Pix Automático guide](https://marketing.iugu.com/guiapixautomatico)
- Vindi launched Pix Automático for recurring billing (SaaS, gyms, subscriptions). An older guide says Vindi has no monthly fee, with a Pix minimum of R$2 per transaction. — [Let's Money](https://www.letsmoney.com.br/noticias/vindi-passa-oferecer-pix-automatico-cobrancas-recorrentes/); [nfe.io](https://nfe.io/blog/financeiro/pix-vindi/)

**Stripe, Paddle, dLocal/EBANX (if selling from abroad or wanting one global stack)**
- Stripe card fee in Brazil is 3.99% + R$0.39 domestic, +2% international (third-party calculator, checked 12 Oct 2026). — [Sellfy](https://sellfy.com/pt/ferramentas/calculadora-de-taxas-do-stripe/)
- Stripe added recurring Pix through subscriptions with mandates (Pix Automático) in April 2026. Pix works on Connect for direct, destination and separate charges, but on Connect only when connected accounts are in the platform's country. An older Stripe page said Pix Automático was unavailable, so check the live docs. — [Stripe changelog 2026-04-22](https://docs.stripe.com/changelog/dahlia/2026-04-22/pix-recurring-payments-support); [Stripe Pix docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/pix)
- Stripe offers Pix to non-Brazilian businesses through EBANX (2025). — [EBANX press](https://business.ebanx.com/en/press-room/press-releases/stripe-users-can-now-accept-pix-in-brazil-via-ebanx)
- Paddle (merchant of record) supports Pix with no Brazilian bank account needed (cap R$250,000 per transaction). Paddle says it registers, collects and remits CBS/IBS. All-in cost is around 5–6% (Paddle blog) or 5% + 50¢ (third party). No boleto support was confirmed. — [Paddle Pix docs](https://developer.paddle.com/concepts/payment-methods/pix); [Paddle Brazil](https://www.paddle.com/billing/brazil); [Paddle blog](https://www.paddle.com/blog/payments-in-brazil)
- dLocal is quoted at about 3–7% all-inclusive and Mercado Pago at 2–4% by country (third-party, unverified). — [paymentproviders.io](https://paymentproviders.io/compare/mercadopago-vs-dlocal?focus=fees)

**Tax and legal**
- ISS: the STF (Tema 590, ADIs 1.945/5.659) settled that software licensing, standard or custom, pays ISS (item 1.05), not ICMS, and that SaaS is a service. Rates range from 2% to 5% by city (São Paulo 2.9%). Parts of a contract may fall under items 1.03 or 1.07. — [TaxUp](https://taxup.com.br/iss-software-saas-municipio-codigo-retencao/); [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/336152/stf-e-a-nova-posicao-sobre-a-tributacao-de-softwares)
- Tax reform: 2026 is a test year, with CBS at 0.9% and IBS at 0.1% (1% symbolic). Sources conflict on whether it is collected or only reported, with offsetting against PIS/Cofins. Invoices (NFS-e) must carry the new IBS/CBS fields. From 2027, non-resident digital providers (e.g. SaaS) must register and collect CBS/IBS on sales to Brazil. — [Contábeis](https://www.contabeis.com.br/noticias/73889/reforma-tributaria-2026-veja-o-que-muda-na-fase-de-transicao/); [Kintsugi guide](https://trykintsugi.com/pt-br/sales-tax-guides/latam/brazil); [Sphere](https://www.getsphere.com/blog/brazil-tax-reform)
- Tax split payment (the reform's own mechanism, separate from marketplace split): optional from 2027, first only for Pix and boleto between companies. — [Let's Money](https://www.letsmoney.com.br/noticias/split-payment-2027-pix-exclui-cartoes/)
- IOF on foreign purchases: a May 2025 decree raised the rate on international card purchases, including foreign digital subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify, Adobe), from 3.38% to 3.5%. Congress later revoked parts of the decree, and sources conflict on whether the card rate is now 3.5% or 3.38%. A separate decree phases card IOF to zero by 2028. Stripe's Pix docs show customers a 3.5% IOF on international purchases. — [Seu Crédito Digital](https://seucreditodigital.com.br/iof-deixa-cartao-internacional-mais-caro/); [Passageiro de Primeira](https://passageirodeprimeira.com/?p=452753); [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/governo-cria-decreto-para-zerar-iof-do-cartao-ate-2028-veja-calendario/); [Stripe search note](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/pix)
- Paying teachers: an individual without a CNPJ is paid with an RPA (Recibo de Pagamento Autônomo), and the payer withholds INSS (11% cited), IRRF and possibly ISS (5% cited), paying them through GPS/DARF. A teacher with a CNPJ (e.g. MEI) issues a nota fiscal. Sources conflict on whether an MEI must issue an NFS-e to a company: one says it is required when the client is a company, another says always. — [Conta Azul](https://contaazul.com/blog/o-que-e-rpa-como-calcular-e-emitir/); [Neon](https://neon.com.br/aprenda/mei/o-que-e-rpa/); [Senhor Contábil](https://senhorcontabil.com.br/blog/emissao-rpa-nota-fiscal-cpf/)
- MEI limit in 2026 is still R$81,000/year (R$6,750/month). A bill to raise it to R$130,000 had not passed. Only listed occupations (Annex XI, Resolução CGSN 140/2018) are allowed. The search did not confirm whether a commission or referral occupation is on the list. — [Contabilidade.com](https://contabilidade.com/blog/mei-em-2026-o-que-e-quem-pode-limite-impostos-e-quando-vale-a-pena); [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/blog/abrir-sua-empresa/faturamento-do-mei/)

### Inferences
- A practical stack for a Brazilian company: Asaas (or Iugu/Vindi) subscriptions with card and Pix Automático, one subaccount per teacher, and a percentage split to the teacher's walletId. The money then goes to the teacher straight from the payment provider, and the app never holds it. This removes most of the "pay commissions to individuals" paperwork for each payment, but the tax nature of the teacher's income still needs an accountant's view. Split at the provider does not by itself settle whether the platform must issue an RPA or receive an NFS-e.
- Opening a subaccount (KYC) is a hurdle for teachers who are not tech-savvy. Alternatives: (a) start with the app collecting everything and paying teachers once a month by Pix against an MEI nota or an RPA (simpler at first, more admin later); (b) use Hotmart/Kiwify co-production for teacher-led sign-ups. Hotmart already knows how to split and pay out to Brazilians, at a cost of about 9.9% + R$1–2.49 per sale.
- Fee arithmetic for an R$24.90 monthly charge (illustrative): Asaas Pix about R$1.99 (8%), Asaas card about R$1.23 (2.99% + R$0.49 = 4.9%), Mercado Pago card about R$1.24 (4.99%), Iugu Pix about R$0.25 (0.99%) plus a plan fee. Annual plans cut the fixed per-charge costs a lot, and a flat Pix fee hurts more on low monthly prices.
- Selling through a foreign entity (Stripe US, Paddle) adds IOF for the Brazilian buyer (about 3.38–3.5% on international cards) and from 2027 CBS/IBS registration. A Brazilian entity charging in BRL avoids IOF for the customer and is the natural route for split to Brazilian teachers (Stripe Connect Pix needs same-country accounts).
- For Spanish-speaking LatAm, Mercado Pago subscriptions (MX, AR, CO, CL) or dLocal/EBANX are the regional options. Teacher split there would need country-specific accounts. A phased plan (Brazil first, LatAm later via a merchant of record) looks realistic.

### Gaps
- The search found no merchant fee for Pix Automático at any provider, and no official current tariff pages (Asaas, Pagar.me, Vindi, Mercado Pago split) could be fetched.
- No source combines Mercado Pago subscriptions with split. Asaas's own docs conflict on subscription + split, and the search did not establish whether split works with Asaas Pix Automático.
- The current IOF rate on international cards (3.38% vs 3.5%) is unresolved.
- Whether teacher commissions count as "intermediação de negócios" for ISS and income tax, and which MEI occupation (CNAE) fits, needs an accountant. No authoritative source was found.

## 4. Price benchmarks in Brazil and purchasing-power pricing

### Takeaway
Brazilian consumer app subscriptions cluster at about R$15–50/month. Canva Pro is R$34.90/month or about R$290/year. Super Duolingo is R$31.90/month or R$179.90/year (about R$15/month). Spotify Individual is R$23.90 and Netflix Standard R$51.90 (Oct 2026). Global quilt tools charge about $8–10/month in USD. PPP guides suggest Brazil prices 20–56% below US list. An app price of about R$19.90–29.90/month, with an annual plan at about R$149–199, fits the market. It is also small next to a patchwork class fee of about R$150/month.

## 5. How big is the teacher channel: teachers and ateliers in Brazil, class sizes and course lengths

### Takeaway
The search found no official count of patchwork teachers or ateliers in Brazil. What can be cited is the shape of the channel: Senac SP's "Confeccionador de Patchwork" course is 160 hours, a cooperative atelier runs weekly 2–2.5-hour classes by level with 7–10 students per class at R$150–165/month, and private lessons average about R$40/hour. With the 111-atelier list already compiled, a realistic first estimate is about 10–30 active students per teacher (inference).

### Cited Findings
- Senac SP "Confeccionador de Patchwork": 160 hours, age 16+, at least lower secondary schooling. The page shows "R$414.40/year" or 12 × R$34.53, but also says to contact the unit for prices. The same figure appears on another course page, so it may be a template value. — [Senac SP](https://www.sp.senac.br/cursos-livres/curso-de-confeccionador-de-patchwork)
- Sicoob/Cressem cooperative courses: patchwork with teacher Silvana on Mondays, 13–15h (beginner), 15–17h (intermediate), 18–20h (advanced), R$150/month, class of 10. Another class costs R$165/month with 7 people. Corte & Costura costs R$180/month. The page is undated. — [Sicoob Cressem](https://www.sicoob.com.br/web/cressem/cursos-oficinas)
- A social-assistance unit in Tocantins ran free patchwork classes in two groups of 10. — [Conexão Tocantins](https://conexaoto.com.br/2017/01/06/projeto-apoiando-e-acreditando-nas-familias-do-tocantins-abre-inscricoes-para-cursos-gratuitos-de-artesanato)
- Private patchwork lessons average R$40/hour (Superprof, Presidente Prudente). GetNinjas lists patchwork teachers by city and state across Brazil. — [Superprof](https://www.superprof.com.br/aulas/patchwork/presidente-prudente/); [GetNinjas RS](https://www.getninjas.com.br/categoria/aulas/moda/patchwork/rs)
- Curitiba's Liceu de Ofícios Criativos offers free patchwork workshops, so public free classes compete for beginners. — [Prefeitura de Curitiba](https://www.curitiba.pr.gov.br/noticias/patchwork-croche-e-scrapbooking-estao-nas-oficinas-gratuitas-do-liceu-de-oficios-criativos/79722)
- Short online courses exist too (e.g. a 15-hour patchwork baby-kit course from Instituto Monitor). — [Instituto Monitor PDF](https://institutomonitor.com.br/PDFPrograma/PATCHWORK%20%20KIT%20PARA%20BEBES%20%20ONLINE.PDF)
- History: the Brazilian patchwork movement started in the early 1990s with middle-class women's "clubinhos de patchwork". Events include the Brazil Patchwork Show (10th edition in 2016) and the Festival Brasileiro de Patchwork e Quilt in Gramado. — [Redalyc academic article](https://www.redalyc.org/journal/273/27370435029/27370435029.pdf); [Anais do Museu Paulista via DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/c62b7da78e5c4710b921e681ad01c15f)

### Inferences
- Students per teacher (illustrative): one atelier teacher running 3 weekly level-classes of 7–10 students has about 20–30 students at a time. A smaller atelier with one class has about 7–10. Not every student will want design software, so a 30–50% uptake is an assumption to test with pilot ateliers, which suggests about 5–15 paying students per teacher at first.
- Teacher income from the share (illustrative): at R$24.90/month and a 25% share, about R$6.20 per student per month. Ten students give about R$62/month and 30 give about R$187/month, roughly one student's class fee. That is a pleasant bonus but not a reason to switch tools on its own, so the free teacher account and classroom features (sharing a block design with the whole class, printing templates) must carry the pitch.
- Channel size (illustrative): 111 known ateliers × about 10 paying students = about 1,100 subscribers, about R$27k/month gross at R$24.90, or about R$20k after a 25% teacher share and before fees and tax. Reaching more teachers through Senac units, cooperatives, festivals (Gramado, Brazil Patchwork Show) and fabric shops would be needed to grow beyond that.
- Course length matters for churn. Senac's 160 h course spans months, and weekly atelier classes are ongoing monthly memberships ("mensalidade"), which suits a monthly subscription better than a one-off course.

### Gaps
- No count of patchwork teachers, ateliers or practitioners in Brazil was found (no association or IBGE data surfaced). The 111-atelier list compiled separately is the best available base.
- No data on what share of patchwork students use computers or phones for design, or on typical retention in atelier classes.
- Spanish-speaking LatAm teacher counts and class formats were not researched (search budget).
