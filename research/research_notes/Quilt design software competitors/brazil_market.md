# Brazilian and Latin American market for quilt design software (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: WebFetch was blocked in this environment (DNS failure on the first site, www.brazilpatchworkshow.com.br), so every finding below comes from WebSearch result text and snippets, not from reading full pages. 35 searches were used. Search results for Portuguese-language software were sparse and noisy. Treat any "not found" as "not found by search snippets", not as proof that something doesn't exist. Instagram/YouTube follower counts could not be retrieved at all (see Gaps).

## 1. Does any quilt design software exist in Portuguese/Spanish, or made in Brazil/Portugal/Latin America?

### Takeaway
No Brazilian, Portuguese or Latin American quilt design program turned up in searches. The only Portuguese-language options found were (a) mySewnet (Husqvarna/Singer group, Sweden), which has a pt-BR product page, though it's unclear whether the quilting program's own screens are in Portuguese; (b) a free AI-company web tool (WaveSpeed) that turns a photo into a square-grid pattern; and (c) Portuguese-language reviews of US tools (PreQuilt) written for search traffic. The market for a real block-based designer in Portuguese, using metric units, looks empty.

### Cited Findings
- Searching "software de patchwork em português" returned almost only dictionary and craft-meaning pages, with no Portuguese quilt design program — [infoescola](https://www.infoescola.com/artes/patchwork); [creativefabrica pt](https://www.creativefabrica.com/pt/product/patchwork-3/)
- WaveSpeed AI offers a free Portuguese-language "Gerador de moldes de patchwork": it turns an image into a quilt of squares, with quilt size and finished square size set by the user. It produces a numbered chart and a cutting list with yardage as a PDF, runs in the browser ("as imagens ficam no seu dispositivo"), and doesn't include borders ("bordas não são incluídas; planeje-as à parte") — [WaveSpeed AI pt](https://wavespeed.ai/pt/free-tools/quilt-pattern-generator)
- mySewnet Quilting Software has a Brazilian Portuguese product page (pt-BR) listing Version 4.1 for Windows and Mac with Portuguese download labels. The page doesn't say whether the software's own screens are in Portuguese. Spanish (es-es, es-mx) pages also exist — [mySewnet pt-BR](https://mysewnet.com/pt-br/Quilting/Quilting-Software); [mySewnet es-mx](https://mysewnet.com/es-mx/Quilting/Quilting%20Software); [mySewnet es-es](https://mysewnet.com/es-es/Quilting/Quilting-Software)
- mySewnet's separate Embroidery software release notes say it "can now be installed in Portuguese". That's the embroidery product, not the quilting one — [mySewnet release notes 14.0](https://softwarehelp.mysewnet.com/ReleaseNotes/mySewnet140.txt)
- mySewnet Quilting covers block design through fabric cutting and sewing. It runs on Windows/Mac desktops and as an Android tablet app, and Windows needs Windows 10, 4 GB RAM and an internet connection — [mySewnet es-es](https://mysewnet.com/es-es/Quilting/Quilting-Software)
- The US page lists mySewnet Quilting at version 4.4, while the pt-BR page shows 4.1, which suggests the Portuguese page lags behind — [mySewnet US](https://cms.prod.mysewnet.com/en-us/Quilting/Quilting-Software); [mySewnet pt-BR](https://mysewnet.com/pt-br/Quilting/Quilting-Software)
- A Portuguese review "PreQuilt vale a pena? Testamos o programa para patchwork" (Omid Saffari blog, Sept 2026, also in Spanish and Italian) says: PreQuilt runs in the browser and lets you test layouts, recolour blocks and estimate fabric. The Basic plan costs US$50/year. The free demo wouldn't save the 20-inch test quilt. The yardage calculator is paid and only works on quilts made in PreQuilt. The review calls Quiltler "the strongest free option for saving" and EQ8 "more complete for technical drawing on desktop". Note that this is a multilingual blog written to rank in search, not a Brazilian quilting source — [omidsaffari.com pt-br](https://omidsaffari.com/pt-br/blog/programa-para-patchwork-prequilt); [omidsaffari.com es](https://omidsaffari.com/es/blog/programa-de-diseno-de-patchwork-prequilt)
- The Spanish search "software de quilting en español" also found only mySewnet (es) plus English tools (EQ, Pro-Stitcher). No Spanish-made or Latin American program turned up — [mySewnet es-es](https://mysewnet.com/es-es/Quilting/Quilting-Software); [Pro-Stitcher](https://prostitcher.com/?p=1587)
- Searching for programs from Argentina/Mexico found nothing local, only mySewnet es-es, BERNINA Quilter and EQ8 — [mySewnet es-es](https://mysewnet.com/es-es/Quilting/Quilting-Software); [WeAllSew BERNINA Quilter](https://weallsew.com/?p=87733)
- Searching "aplicativo para criar patchwork celular" found no Portuguese mobile quilt design app, only games, AI image sites and a Windows Store quilt app — [Microsoft Store quilt app](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9p610vbzpvg6?hl=en-US)
- No Portuguese-language fabric-yardage/patchwork calculator app turned up on Google Play. The apps that did turn up (Quilt Geek, Quilt cat, Quilting Calculators, QuiltingCalc) are in English. Omni Calculator has a generic Portuguese web fabric calculator — [Omni Calculator pt](https://www.omnicalculator.com/pt/dia-a-dia/calculadora-tecido); [QuiltingCalc App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/quiltingcalc/id390366459)
- Quiltler (iPad/iOS, US developer): Quiltler 2 is free with in-app purchases. Quiltler 3 needs a subscription after a 7-day trial and links to US fabric sources (Missouri Star, Robert Kaufman). The original Quiltler costs US$9.99 and was last updated in Dec 2019 — [Quiltler 2 App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1495594391); [Quiltler 3 App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6755831082); [appshunter](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/1120958195/reviews)
- An academic Japanese system, "Patchy" (Igarashi and Mitani, SIGGRAPH 2015 poster), is in English and Japanese only — [Patchy](https://www.is.ocha.ac.jp/~yuki/patchy/index-e.html)

### Inferences
- The only Portuguese-labelled "quilt software" with real block design is mySewnet, a sewing-machine company's product sold mainly through its dealers. It isn't positioned as a free, browser-based, metric tool. A free pt-BR browser designer with metric cutting and real-size A4 templates would have no direct local competitor found by this research.
- WaveSpeed's tool and the Omid Saffari reviews show that companies outside Brazil already go after Portuguese searches like "programa para patchwork". Search engine optimisation (SEO) in Portuguese, for example a page titled "programa para patchwork grátis", is a realistic way in and is already contested by generic AI-tool and review sites.

### Gaps
- Couldn't confirm whether EQ8's screens offer Portuguese or Spanish. Search found no language list. Background knowledge (unverified here): EQ8 is English-only but lets you work in centimetres. This should be checked on electricquilt.com.
- Couldn't confirm whether mySewnet Quilting or BERNINA Quilter offer Portuguese screens.
- Couldn't search Google Play or the App Store directly. A small local app (for example a "calculadora de patchwork") could exist without showing up in web search.

## 2. Do Brazilian teachers/schools teach EQ8 or other software? What do they charge?

### Takeaway
No Portuguese-language EQ8 course, Brazilian EQ8 teacher or Mercado Livre/Hotmart software course turned up. Brazilian patchwork teaching is about sewing technique (Senac, Hotmart, eduK, Skillshare-pt), not digital design. EQ8 training exists only in English (EQ Academy, US guilds, CTPub) at US prices.

### Cited Findings
- "Curso EQ8 em português" found no Portuguese course. The courses that came up are in English: EQ Academy (half-day 3 h / full-day 6 h classes), "Getting Started with EQ8" 2-day series, and CTPub Creative Spark "Electric Quilt Software Basics" (Jennifer Rapacki) at US$49.99, software not included — [EQ Academy](https://classes.electricquilt.com/class-catalog/); [EQ8 2-day series](https://classes.electricquilt.com/class-catalog/getting-started-with-eq8-2-day-series); [CTPub Creative Spark](https://creativespark.ctpub.com/courses/eq8-software-basics)
- Senac São Paulo runs "Confeccionador de Patchwork", a 160-hour course on sewing techniques, colour composition and finishing. The snippet doesn't mention any design software — [Senac SP](https://www.sp.senac.br/cursos-livres/curso-de-confeccionador-de-patchwork)
- Hotmart sells "Curso básico de Patchwork" by Graça Freitas, who describes 19 years in "artesanato lucrativo" (crafts as income). No software course turned up — [Hotmart](https://hotmart.com/pt-br/marketplace/produtos/curso-basico-de-patchwork/W77194113F)
- eduK (Brazilian online craft school) offers patchwork courses with artisan Márcia Satiko (bags and accessories, traditional patchwork). It had a catalogue of 600+ courses during a free promotion with Catraca Livre, and eduK + Elo7 ran a free online patchwork course — [Catraca Livre](https://catracalivre.com.br/educacao/curso-online-gratuito-patchwork/); [ABCdoABC](https://abcdoabc.com.br/eduk-e-elo7-oferecem-curso-de-patchwork-gratuito-pela-internet-2/)
- Skillshare has a Portuguese-translated beginner patchwork/quilting class (Brigitte Heitland) and a Portuguese-listed class on making patchwork *surface patterns* in Procreate/Photoshop (Kristina Hultkrantz) — [Skillshare pt Heitland](https://www.skillshare.com/pt/classes/nocoes-basicas-de-patchwork-e-quilting-como-costurar-uma-capa-de-almofada-exclusiva/1929811610); [Skillshare pt Procreate](https://www.skillshare.com/pt/classes/3-padroes-de-patchwork-no-procreate-e-no-photoshop/1097433595)
- Brazilian TV channel Canal Rural ("Arte Brasil" segments) has many patchwork how-to segments with instructors such as Silvia Pondaco. These are sewing techniques, not design tools — [Canal Rural](https://www.canalrural.com.br/entretenimento/arte-brasil/parte-patchwork-embutido-23128/)
- A Brazilian how-to guide tells beginners to "desenhar o projeto primeiro no caderno quadriculado e depois providenciar os cortes" (draw on graph paper first, then cut) — [Decor Fácil](https://www.decorfacil.com/como-fazer-patchwork)
- Tilda (a Norwegian fabric brand) publishes free quilt patterns as Portuguese PDFs ("…-pt.pdf"), with layouts and cut measurements per block — [Tilda Colour Dance Quilt pt](https://www.tildasworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Colour-Dance-Quilt-pt.pdf); [Tilda Dear Santa Quilt pt](https://www.tildasworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Dear-Santa-Quilt-plaid-stone-Po.pdf)
- Spanish-speaking Latin America has public and university patchwork courses: Buenos Aires city government's "Introducción al patchwork" (PDF dated Aug 2026) and Universidad de América (Colombia) patchwork course (2024 brochure). The snippets don't mention design software — [Buenos Aires gob](https://static.buenosaires.gob.ar/sites/default/files/2026-08/Introducci%C3%B3n%20al%20patchwork.pdf); [Universidad de América](https://www.uamerica.edu.co/wp-content/documentos/Brochure/Folleto%20_%20Curso%20Patchwork%2028062024.pdf)

### Inferences
- Brazilian quilters seem to design with graph paper, printed patterns (often translated foreign ones like Tilda) and teachers' handouts, not software. That matches what the owner already suspected (graph paper / Excel), but no source found confirms Excel or Canva use specifically.
- Teachers are an obvious channel. A free tool that prints metric cutting plans and A4 templates could be used *by teachers* to make their course handouts. No competitor serves that in Portuguese.

### Gaps
- No prices for Brazilian EQ8 or digital-design courses, because none were found.
- No evidence either way about Excel, Canva or PowerPoint use by Brazilian quilters. This would need a survey or a search of Facebook groups (not reachable here).

## 3. How do competitors serve Brazil (Portuguese UI, BRL prices, payment, import costs, metric)?

### Takeaway
The main competitors sell in US dollars only, with no Brazilian reseller or Mercado Livre listing found. A Brazilian buyer pays the dollar price plus 3.5% IOF (a federal tax on foreign card purchases) plus the card's exchange-rate margin. EQ8 at ~US$240 is a large outlay in Brazil, and PreQuilt is a US$50/year subscription.

### Cited Findings
- EQ8 is listed at US$239.95 on the official shop and at eQuilter, US$224.89 at Fabric Depot, and £228 in the UK (EQ7→EQ8 upgrade £93.99) — [Electric Quilt shop](https://electricquilt.com/online-shop/category/electric-quilt-8-eq8); [eQuilter](https://www.equilter.com/product/241364/eq8-complete-quilt-design-software-mac-+-pc); [Fabric Depot](https://fabricdepot.com/products/software-electric-quilt-8-quilt-design-eq8); [Search Press](https://trade.searchpress.com/book/9781800920101/electric-quilt-8-eq8-upgrade-quilt-design-software)
- No BRL price and no Brazilian seller of EQ8 turned up. A Mercado Livre search found no EQ8 listing — [search result: EQ8 retailers](https://www.shabbyfabrics.com/p160538/eq8-electric-quilt-8-quilt-design-software)
- PreQuilt Basic costs US$50/year. The free demo doesn't save — [omidsaffari.com es](https://omidsaffari.com/es/blog/programa-de-diseno-de-patchwork-prequilt)
- IOF on international card purchases (credit, debit, prepaid) has been a single 3.5% rate since 17 July 2025 (Decree 12.499/2025). It was 3.38% from 2 Jan 2025, and the rise followed Ministry of Finance announcements on 22 May 2025. It's charged on the amount in reais at the day's rate — [InvestNews](https://investnews.com.br/economia/fechou-o-cerco-iof-vai-a-35-para-cambio-com-cartoes-de-credito-debito-e-pre-pagos-internacionais/amp/); [ArqFinance 2026 table](https://arqfinance.com/articles/?p=2031); [DGABC](https://www.dgabc.com.br/Noticia/4195195/iof-sobre-compras-com-cartao-de-credito-no-exterior-cai-para-3-38-neste-ano)
- Patchwork rulers are sold in Brazil in both inches (6 × 24 in, 1/8 in marks, 1/4 in seam line) and centimetres (10 × 45 cm, 2.5 mm marks) — [Ponto Frio inch ruler](https://www.pontofrio.com.br/regua-patchwork-6x24-polegadas/p/1541011129); [Ponto Frio cm ruler](https://www.pontofrio.com.br/regua-patchwork-10x45cm-cristal/p/1541010619)
- A Brazilian buying guide advises using an inch ruler when the pattern is in inches "para evitar erros de arredondamento" (to avoid rounding errors) and a metric ruler for metric patterns. That implies both systems are in use and converting between them is a known nuisance — [Busca Melhores](https://buscamelhores.com.br/qual-a-melhor-regua-para-patchwork)
- PreQuilt's Portuguese review describes its test quilt as "20 polegadas" (20 inches), so the US tools' inch-first habits carry over even into Portuguese write-ups — [omidsaffari.com pt-br](https://omidsaffari.com/pt-br/blog/programa-para-patchwork-prequilt)

### Inferences
- Rough cost to a Brazilian buyer: US$239.95 × the exchange rate + 3.5% IOF + the card's margin. The exchange rate at time of reading wasn't retrieved, so no BRL figure is given. For comparison, a half-metre of local patchwork cotton costs ~R$30–39 (see section 4), so EQ8 equals dozens of fabric cuts.
- Competitors don't price locally or accept local payment methods. Pix, boleto and payment in instalments ("parcelado") weren't mentioned in any competitor result, and they are the normal ways Brazilians pay. If the owner ever charges, local payment through Mercado Pago, Hotmart or Pix would set the app apart. That's an inference, not a cited finding.
- Brazil uses both metric and inch rulers. So "metric only" is a strength for local patterns and fabric sold by the metre, but the tool may also need to show or accept inches for people following US patterns or using inch rulers.

### Gaps
- Couldn't confirm whether Electric Quilt Co. accepts Brazilian cards or charges extra. EQ8 is a download, so no customs duty would apply, but this wasn't confirmed.
- Exchange rate (BRL/USD) at Nov 2026 not retrieved.

## 4. Size and shape of the Brazilian patchwork/quilting community (events, schools, fabric industry, magazines, influencers)

### Takeaway
Brazil has a lively and long-running circuit of patchwork events. Brazil Patchwork Show in São Paulo has run since at least 2007 (10th edition in 2016). Floripa Quilt is in its 13th edition (2026). QuiltBrasil in Gramado reached 28 editions and paused in 2025. Limeira Patchwork (14th edition) has 74 exhibitors and 80 coach groups. Fabric comes from national mills (Fernando Maluhy, Karsten Ateliê) and online marketplaces, and there are several patchwork magazines. No hard numbers on visitors, the number of practitioners or influencers' followers could be found.

### Inferences
- The community is centred in the South and Southeast (SP, SC, RS, PR, RJ, MG). Events double as markets and class venues, and coach groups (caravanas) suggest organised groups and ateliers. Those are natural places to demonstrate a free tool (a stand, a workshop, or a QR code in a class handout).
- Brazilian patchwork blends quilting with "artesanato" products (bags, rag dolls, cushions) and crafts as a source of income ("artesanato lucrativo"). Many people may care more about small projects and selling than about bed-size quilt design. The app's block-by-block bed quilt focus fits the quilt-show crowd better than the bag/accessory crowd.
- QuiltBrasil's pause after 28 editions, if confirmed, may mean the big traditional festival is weakening or changing format, while regional events (Floripa, Limeira/Americana) carry on.

- **No follower/subscriber counts** for Brazilian patchwork YouTube channels or Instagram teachers. - Couldn't find "Ottoboni" fabrics in search results, or any official detail on Peripan or Fabricart.
- No figures on Brazilian quilt guilds or associations (e.g. whether a national patchwork club exists and how many members it has).

## 5. Pain points and unmet needs (price in dollars, inches vs metric, English-only software)

### Takeaway
The evidence suggests three unmet needs: (1) no quilt *design* tool with Portuguese screens built for Brazil; (2) tools priced in dollars (EQ8 ~US$240 one-off, PreQuilt US$50/year, Quiltler 3 subscription) plus 3.5% IOF; (3) inch-first patterns and tools, while local fabric is sold in metres and rulers come in both systems. Beginners are told to plan on graph paper and work out cuts by hand, which is exactly what the owner's app automates (cutting plan, real-size A4 templates). Direct statements from Brazilian quilters about these pains weren't found.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- The owner's app matches several gaps found: free, browser-based (no Windows/iPad needed), metric-first, a cutting plan plus real-size A4 PDF templates (Brazil uses A4, not US Letter), and the user's own fabric photos. That last point matters because local fabrics (Fernando Maluhy, Karsten, Peripan) aren't in US fabric libraries such as Quiltler's Missouri Star or Robert Kaufman links. All of this is inferred from the absence of competitors, not from quilters saying so.
- Portuguese screens (pt-BR) look like the single most important requirement. Spanish (es-419) would open Argentina, Mexico and Colombia, where only mySewnet has Spanish pages.
- Options to consider: showing inches next to metric (for US patterns and inch rulers), fabric sold in 1.40 m widths and half-metre cuts in the fabric calculator, and printable handouts for teachers and ateliers.
