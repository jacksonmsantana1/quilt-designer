# Desktop (Windows/Mac) quilt design software: competitors

Research date: 2 November 2026. Method note: direct page fetching was blocked in this environment (electricquilt.com failed to resolve), so every finding below comes from web-search result text. Each claim cites the URL the search showed. Many "best quilt software 2026" listicles (gitnux.org, worldmetrics.org, zipdo.co, wifitalents.com, ddg.wcroc.umn.edu, several *.netlify.app pages) look auto-generated or keyword-stuffed. They are cited only where they add something and are flagged as low-trust. Prices are retailer snapshots unless marked "official". None of them were confirmed as current list prices for late 2026.

## Which desktop quilt design programs exist, and what does each offer?

### Takeaway
Electric Quilt 8 (EQ8) is the only actively maintained, full-featured desktop quilt designer for both Windows and Mac. Most other names are small, Windows-only, frozen for years, tied to a sewing-machine brand, or really longarm stitch-path tools rather than piecing designers. No desktop product had evidence of a Portuguese or Spanish interface, and none had evidence of a 3D drape preview.

### Inferences
- The desktop market is effectively one serious product (EQ8) plus a long tail:
  - Brand-tied tools: mySewnet, Brother, Quilters Select/RNK
  - Frozen products: Quilt-Pro, Quilt Assistant
  - Longarm stitch tools priced $400–$1,000+
- Lusophone and Hispanic quilters appear to have no native-language desktop option. A Portuguese and Spanish metric app would face almost no direct desktop competition in Brazil and Latin America.
- Names in the brief that are not separate current products:
  - "Patchdraw" is EQ's appliqué drawing mode, not a standalone program.
  - "Quilt Wizard" is Quilt-Pro's quilt module (an unrelated iOS app also uses the name).
  - "Quilter's Design Studio" most likely means Grace's Quilter's Creative Design.
- Several products need an internet connection to activate (EQ Mini, mySewnet). The owner's offline single HTML file has no such dependency.

## What is EQ8's position as the market leader?

### Takeaway
EQ8 is the default recommendation in forums and retailer catalogues. It is a one-off, $240-list desktop licence on Windows and Mac. The current major version shipped in November 2017, Windows has had no update since March 2021, and Mac updates have mostly been compatibility fixes (latest found November 2023). Users praise its power and support but repeatedly mention the time it takes to learn. No cloud, browser or mobile version, and no EQ9, has been announced.

### Cited Findings
- **Company and history**
  - Founded by Dean Neumann (former BGSU maths professor, who wrote the software) and Penny McMorris (quilt TV producer). It debuted on her WBGU-TV "Great American Quilt" programme in 1991; another source says 1992 — [EQ About](https://electricquilt.com/home/about-electric-quilt/), [While She Naps podcast #93](https://whileshenaps.com/podcast-episode-93-penny-mcmorris-dean-neumann-co-founders-electric-quilt/)
  - Over 54 software editions and 27+ books — [Moda Fabrics profile](https://my.modafabrics.com/node/31813)
  - Calls itself "the leading quilt-design software in the world for over 30 years" (self-description) — [EQ Quilt Design Wizard page](https://electricquilt.com/online-shop/quilt-design-wizard)
- **Market position**
  - "The most popular quilt software on the market now is Electric Quilt version 8" — [Stitch in Heaven (2021)](https://stitchinheaven.com/blogs/howdy/the-most-popular-quilt-design-software-options)
  - Content Mavericks names it "best overall" at $239.95; Gitnux gives it 9.2/10 (low trust) — [Content Mavericks](https://contentmavericks.com/best-quilt-design-software/), [Gitnux](https://gitnux.org/best/quilt-design-software/)
  - A public library bought EQ8 for patrons, a sign of institutional standing — [WCDPL](https://wcdpl.org/node/14540)
- **Update cadence**
  - EQ5 → EQ6 → EQ7 (shipped 7 June 2010) → EQ8 (November 2017) — [Art Quilt Maker blog](https://artquiltmaker.com/blog/2010/04/28/), [Christa Quilts](https://christaquilts.com/2017/11/24/eq8-has-now-been-released-and-you-can-win-a-copy/comment-page-1)
  - Windows 1.1.0.3 (March 2021); Mac 3.2.0.7 (November 2023) — [EQ8 updates](https://electricquilt.com/product-updates/eq8-updates/), [release notes](https://electricquilt.com/product-updates/eq8-updates/eq8-update-release-notes/)
  - No EQ9 found. The only 2026 product news found was the Quilt Journeys add-on and an "EQ For You Starter Box" distributor listing with an expected release of 14 April 2026 — [Barbara Brackman](https://barbarabrackman.blogspot.com/2026/01/quilt-journeys-state-blocks-for-eq.html), [Checker Distributors](https://www.checkerdist.com/products/A-EQ8BOX)

  - "It takes a serious commitment of time to learn"; one user "never did learn it" and found it "not intuitive"; others say it is not hard and EQ8 is more intuitive than earlier versions — [Quiltingboard](https://www.quiltingboard.com/showthread.php?p=8296666), [Quiltingboard](https://www.quiltingboard.com/main-f1/eq8-im-considering-t303283-2.html)
  - Price: users wait for sales ("Quiltinaday usually has the best price"); "if you aren't interested in designing a quilt, it might not be worth your $$" — [Quiltingboard EQ8 on sale](https://www.quiltingboard.com/showthread.php?p=8575163), [Quiltingboard](https://www.quiltingboard.com/printfriendly-eq8-t309770.html)

  - A paid ecosystem of books and courses exists largely because of the learning curve — [eQuilter](https://www.equilter.com/product/290181/eq8-lessons-for-beginners-a-step-by-step-exercise-book), [C&T Creative Spark](https://creativespark.ctpub.com/courses/eq8-software-basics)
- **Licensing**
  - Two simultaneous activations per licence; moving to a new computer can mean buying a $24.95 Migration Kit — [EQ support](https://support.electricquilt.com/?p=11151), [EQ support: no more licences](https://support.electricquilt.com/?p=1121), [EQ shop](https://electricquilt.com/online-shop/eq8-migration-kit)

### Inferences
- EQ8 is a nine-year-old major version on a slow, maintenance-only cadence. The company sells content (add-ons, books, classes) more than new software. That leaves room for a modern, free, browser-based tool, especially for casual quilters who find EQ8 too costly and too slow to learn.
- Dependence on Rosetta, if true, is a medium-term risk for EQ8 on Mac once Apple drops Rosetta or Intel support. A browser app has no such exposure.
- EQ8's moat is depth: thousands of blocks, manufacturer fabric libraries, foundation piecing and 30 years of tutorials. A newcomer with 27 blocks competes on ease, price, offline browser use, 3D realism and language, not on library size.

### Gaps
- No source gave EQ's user base, sales or revenue.
- No official statement on cloud sync, mobile apps or EQ9. Absence in search results is not proof that none is planned.
- No Reddit r/quilting threads surfaced; all forum evidence is from Quiltingboard.
- Specific complaints about a "dated UI", no cloud or no mobile were not found in the search text, so they remain unconfirmed. Only the learning curve and price were directly evidenced.

## Which programs are discontinued or abandoned?

### Takeaway
Most of the historical field is gone or frozen:
- **Discontinued:** PCQuilt (the first, 1986), QuiltSOFT, Quilt Design Wizard (EQ's own beginner product), Grace QuiltCAD and Brother's Dream Motion.
- **Effectively abandoned:** Quilt-Pro and Quilt Assistant.
- **Old platform:** Quilt Fusion's desktop build depends on Adobe AIR.

### Cited Findings
- **PCQuilt**
  - Nina Antze built it for IBM PCs in 1986 and is credited with the first quilt design software — [Quilt Study "American Story"](https://worldquilts.quiltstudy.org/americanstory/node/130)
  - Shareware on Compuserve by 1993 — [discmaster 1993 Usenet archive](https://discmaster.textfiles.com/view/28249/NN_1993_3.iso/spool/rec/crafts/textiles/2652)
  - Lingered as "PCQuilter" with a largely inactive Yahoo group; no current site found — [Quiltingboard](https://www.quiltingboard.com/6265029-post17.html)
- **QuiltSOFT**: on the market by the late 1990s; Windows; version 6.0 bundled Marsha McCloskey and Beckie Olson block libraries (low-trust page); no current site, version or price found — [Quilt Study](https://worldquilts.quiltstudy.org/americanstory/node/130), [WifiTalents](https://wifitalents.com/best/quilt-computer-software/)
- **Quilt Design Wizard** (Electric Quilt): beginner program for the EQ6/7 era; "no longer sold by The Electric Quilt Company"; no hexagons, no photos, could not open full EQ projects — [EQ shop](https://electricquilt.com/online-shop/quilt-design-wizard), [Sewing Products blog, Feb 2011](https://sewingproducts.blogspot.com/2011/02/electric-quilt-co-quilt-design-wizard.html)
- **Quilt-Pro**
  - "Hasn't been updated since 2018"; a blog says "no longer in service" and a commenter says "the Quilt Pro site is closed and no longer working" (unverified, undated) — [Patchwork Posse](https://www.patchworkposse.com/quilt-pro/), [Patchwork Posse](https://www.patchworkposse.com/?p=29435)
  - Users report no response from tech support — [Stitch in Heaven](https://stitchinheaven.com/blogs/howdy/the-most-popular-quilt-design-software-options)
  - The Mac edition stopped at v5 — [Quiltingboard Mac thread](https://quiltingboard.com/main-f1/quilting-software-mac-t105223-5.html)
  - Foundation Factory users struggle to get it running again — [Quiltingboard](https://www.quiltingboard.com/main-f1/quilt-pro-t193910.html?nojs=1)
- **Quilt Assistant**: free; latest version v2.25 from about 2013 — [Free Download Manager](https://en.freedownloadmanager.org/Windows-PC/QuiltAssistant-FREE.html)
- **Grace QuiltCAD**: replaced by Quilter's Creative Design — [Sewing Machines Plus](https://www.sewingmachinesplus.com/quilters-creative-design-software.php), [Sewing Machines Plus QuiltCAD](https://sewingmachinesplus.com/products/grace-quilt-cad-software)
- **Brother "THE Dream Motion" (SAQCDM) and SAQCDMPRO**: marked discontinued — [Brother USA](https://www.brother-usa.com/products/SAQCDM), [Brother USA](https://www.brother-usa.com/products/SAQCDMPRO)
- **Older EQ versions** (EQ4–EQ7): superseded; still resold second-hand (for example sealed EQ7 Mac on eBay.de) — [eBay.de](https://www.ebay.de/itm/205649120735)
- **Quilt Fusion desktop**: needs Adobe AIR and users must sometimes install AIR by hand. Adobe ended its own AIR support in 2020 (this date is from general knowledge, not from a search result) — [Chatterbox Quilts](https://chatterboxquilts.squarespace.com/blog/quilt-fusionthefuture-ofquiltdesign)

### Inferences
- The churn shows that desktop quilt software is hard to sustain for small vendors. Only EQ survived at scale, by selling content and education around the software.
- People leaving Quilt-Pro (Windows-only, unsupported) are a natural audience for a free browser tool.

### Gaps
- No official discontinuation dates for Quilt-Pro, QuiltSOFT or PCQuilt.
- Whether quiltpro.com still sells licences in 2026 could not be checked (fetching was blocked).
- The Adobe AIR end-of-support date was not found in the search results. HARMAN took AIR over afterwards, so check whether Quilt Fusion still runs.

## How does the owner's browser app compare?

### Takeaway
Against desktop incumbents, the owner's app is thin on content (27 blocks against EQ8's 6,700+) but unusual in several ways:
- Free and browser-based, works offline as one file, metric
- Real-size A4 templates
- 3D cloth-physics drape (no desktop rival was found to have one)
- Webcam fabric capture
- Planned cross-device sync

Its clearest openings are price, zero install on any OS, Portuguese/Spanish and metric-first markets, and ease of learning.

### Cited Findings
- **Price.** EQ8 costs about $240 one-off — [Nebraska Quilt Co.](https://www.nebraskaquiltcompany.com/products/electric-quilt-8-quilt-design-software). Design N Quilt costs $200–$400 — [The Quilt Show](https://thequiltshow.com/shop/books-cds-dvds/quilting-software/design-n-quilt-quilting-design-software-from-quilters-select). Longarm design tools cost $480–$995 — [Nebraska Quilt Co.](https://nebraskaquiltcompany.com/products/pro-stitcher-designer), [Sewing Machines Plus](https://sewingmachinesplus.com/products/hq-20807-art-n-stitch-software). The owner's app is free.
- **Platforms.** EQ8 runs on Windows and Mac only. Quilt-Pro 6, Brother SAPVQ, Quilt Assistant and Grace's software are Windows only — [Patchwork Posse](https://www.patchworkposse.com/quilt-pro/), [Brother USA](https://www.brother-usa.com/products/sapvq), [Sewing Machines Plus](https://www.sewingmachinesplus.com/quilters-creative-design-software.php). The owner's app runs in any modern browser, including Chromebooks, Linux and tablets.
- **Library size.** EQ8 has 6,700+ blocks and about 6,200 fabrics — [EQ](https://electricquilt.com/landing-pages/eq8), [Lady Sew and Sew](https://ladysewandsew.co.uk/?p=724029). Quilt-Pro has 1,500 blocks and 3,000 fabrics — [Stitch in Heaven](https://stitchinheaven.com/blogs/howdy/the-most-popular-quilt-design-software-options). Brother has 150+ blocks — [Brother USA](https://www.brother-usa.com/products/sapvq). The owner has 27 blocks with editable parts.
- **Printing.** EQ8 and Quilt-Pro print templates, foundation-piecing patterns and rotary cutting charts — [EQ8](https://electricquilt.com/online-shop/electric-quilt-8/), [Patchwork Posse](https://www.patchworkposse.com/quilt-pro/). The owner has a cutting guide and an A4 PDF with real-size templates. Foundation paper piecing is not listed among the owner's features.
- **Fabric photos.** EQ8 imports photos and scans with crop and straighten tools — [Search Press](https://www.searchpress.com/book/9781800920095/electric-quilt-8-eq8-quilt-design-software). The owner adds file and webcam import, but the UI entry points are currently hidden (per the project notes).
- **Quilting stitches.** Stitch design is a separate paid product category ($400–$1,000+) — [Sewing Machines Plus](https://sewingmachinesplus.com/products/hq-20807-art-n-stitch-software). The owner offers a quilting-stitch preview only.

### Inferences
- **Differentiators with no desktop match found:**
  - A 3D drape on a bed or table with cloth physics
  - Works offline as a single HTML file with no install or activation
  - Metric-first units
  - Free
  - Cross-device sync (once live)
  - A Portuguese or Spanish UI, if the owner localizes; no desktop rival has one
- **Gaps against EQ8 that reviewers value:**
  - Block library depth, custom block drawing (EQ's EasyDraw/PatchDraw)
  - Manufacturer fabric libraries, yardage for several fabric widths
  - Foundation paper piecing printouts, appliqué
  - The tutorial and support ecosystem
- **Positioning.** Present the app as the easy, free, modern alternative for people who find EQ8 too costly or too slow to learn. Do not position it as an EQ8 replacement for professional pattern designers.

### Gaps
- No side-by-side usability test or user survey exists to back up claims that the owner's app is easier.
- Whether the owner's cutting guide covers yardage across fabric widths, as EQ8 does, should be checked against the codebase. It was outside this research.
