# Mobile and tablet quilt design and quilting utility apps (competitors), as of November 2026

Method note: WebFetch was blocked in this environment (apps.apple.com failed to resolve), so no store page was opened directly. Everything below comes from WebSearch result text (35 searches), which quotes App Store listings and third-party trackers (appshunter.io, appgoblin.info, apptail.io, AppBrain, appfollow, similarweb, mwm.ai). Ratings and review counts are therefore snapshots from those sources and may be out of date or differ by storefront. Where a source is a roundup from 2013–2024, that is noted.

## Which mobile/tablet quilt design apps exist?

### Takeaway
The mobile design field is small, fragmented and mostly iOS/iPad: one long-established paid iPad app (Quiltography, $14.99, not updated since ~2016), a handful of older indie iOS apps (Quilt Wizard, Quiltler 1/2, Quilt Fusion, QuiltPaper, Quilt Pattern Creator), and a 2025–2026 wave of new subscription iOS apps (Quiltler 3, QuiltFit, Swatchit, Quilt Duck, TideStitch, Quilter's App). Android has almost nothing for design besides Quilt Cat (3.6 stars, 50k+ installs). No mobile app found offers a 3D drape, real-size printable templates in metric and offline use together; none is from Electric Quilt.

- Review counts are tiny (tens to a few hundred), so this is a niche with no dominant mobile product.

## Which utility apps do quilters use?

### Takeaway
Utility apps are dominated by free fabric-company calculators (Robert Kaufman's QuiltingCalc) and the Missouri Star tutorial/shop app (by far the most reviewed: ~34–37k iOS ratings at 4.9). Newer calculators (Quilt Geek, Quiltry, QuiltYield) use freemium subscriptions. Stash apps are many and small, mostly iPhone-only. Colour tools that match photos to real solids (Quilty Solid, Swatchit's Kona matching, Play Crafts Palette Builder) exist; no official Kona colour card app was found.

### Cited Findings

**Calculators**
- QuiltingCalc (Robert Kaufman Fabrics; calculators by Quilter's Paradise; seller Handmade Interactive LLC): free; fabric conversion, backing/batting, piece counts, binding, borders, setting/corner triangles, square-in-a-square; ~4.2 from ~103 ratings — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quiltingcalc/id390366459); [appshunter](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/390366459)
  - Complaints: no landscape/iPad layout, no yardage-to-metric for some users, opens blank sometimes, calculator disappeared after an update — [App Store via search](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quiltingcalc/id390366459)

- Quilt Geek Quilting Calculator: free with IAP; free binding/backing calculators, paid 20+ calculators and design tools; 4.5 from 84 ratings; complaint "aggressive paywall"; also on Google Play — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6499062474); [appshunter](https://appshunter.io/ios/topics/quilting-tool-app)

### Gaps

## How does the owner's app compare?

### Takeaway
On features the owner's browser app is broader than any single mobile app found (block library + borders/sashing/binding/backing + symmetry + fussy cutting + own fabric photos + quilting-stitch preview + cutting plan + real-size A4 PDF templates in mm + 3D cloth drape + offline + free). Its weaknesses vs. mobile apps are distribution (no store presence, no native app), no login/sync live yet, and fewer blocks than library-heavy apps (27 vs. 100+/200+).

### Cited Findings (competitor side; owner's features are from the brief)
- Block counts: Quilt Wizard 100+ — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id475622761); Quilt Pattern Creator 200+ — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6448708909); PreQuilt 120+ — [Stitched in Color](https://www.stitchedincolor.com/blog/2024/7/3/welcome-prequilt); Quilt Duck free 12 / paid 50+ — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6755566364)
- Real-size printing: only QuiltPaper (exact-size shapes, SVG to cutters) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1139385294); PDF export is paywalled in QuiltFit Pro — [App Store NL](https://apps.apple.com/nl/app/id6776541705)
- Realistic preview: Swatchit room scenes — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6760553382); no 3D cloth drape found in any mobile app.
- Offline/no login as a selling point: QuiltFit, TideStitch — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/bt/app/id6776541705); [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/app/apple/6785949038/)
- Sync: Quiltler 3 (iPhone/iPad), Swatchit (phone/web), Quilt Fusion (cloud) — sources above; Quilt Cat cannot sync — [Patchwork Posse](https://www.patchworkposse.com/sewing-quilting-apps/)
- Price models: one-off (Quiltography $14.99, Quilt Wizard $5.99, photoQuilt $4.99, Quilt Duck unlock); subscription (Quiltler 3, QuiltFit €9.99/month, Quiltry $3.99/month, Quilt Fusion $19.99/month) — sources above

### Inferences
- Unique to the owner's app among mobile competitors found: 3D drape with cloth physics, symmetry editing, fussy cutting, quilting-stitch preview, free real-size metric templates, and (once added) Portuguese UI.
- Recurring complaints to design against: paywalls/limits on fabric count (Quilt Cat, Quilt Duck, Quilt Geek), abandoned apps (Quiltography 2016, Quilt Wizard 2020), clunky controls (Quilt Wizard), photo colour accuracy, lost features after updates (Quiltler 2, QuiltingCalc), no iPad landscape layout (QuiltingCalc).
- The known gap (no visible "Add fabric" button) matters: fabric photos are a baseline feature in competitors.
- A browser app on iPad/Android tablets reaches both platforms at once, where most competitors are iOS-only; touch usability on tablets is worth testing.

### Gaps
- No hands-on comparison of usability; competitor feature claims are mostly from developers' store copy.
