# Web-based quilt design and planning tools (competitors), as of November 2026

Method note: WebFetch was blocked by this session's network policy (the first try, prequilt.com, failed with "host not allowed"), so no pages could be opened in full. Everything below comes from search-result text (35 WebSearch calls). Many claims come from the vendors' own marketing pages, and those are marked as such. A lot of "best quilt software" result pages are SEO/affiliate filler (e.g. pattern.it.com, lbas.lboro.ac.uk, ddg.wcroc.umn.edu, wifitalents, worldmetrics). I did not rely on them except where noted. Prices are in USD.

## Which web-based quilt design / planning tools exist? (catalogue)

### Takeaway
The browser quilt-design space in 2026 is crowded and young. PreQuilt (2018) is the best-known name. Since about 2025 a wave of newer freemium web tools has arrived: Quiltler (web + iOS), NiftyFifty (free, with cutting charts and true-size templates), CINCHQuilt, QuiltButler, Yard, Swatchit and QuiltWall. Alongside them are vendor-locked planners (Quiltster/Quiltworx), older tools that are dead or dormant (Threadbias, PatternJam, MyWebQuilter, Play Crafts Palette Builder), and many single-purpose calculators and AI photo-to-quilt generators. The leading full design tool, Electric Quilt 8, is still desktop-only.

### Cited Findings

**PreQuilt (prequilt.com): the best-known browser design tool**
- A browser app for designing and visualizing quilts. A blogger who sponsors it says she has used it "since its 2018 launch". PreQuilt Coloring Sheets of existing patterns are on the site. — [Stitched in Color, Jul 2024](https://www.stitchedincolor.com/blog/2024/7/3/welcome-prequilt)
- It began as a color explorer that launched "in early July", with full quilt-pattern creation as the longer-term goal (an older post). It has a Product Hunt listing ("design tool for creative quilters", can randomize generative patterns). — [While She Naps](https://whileshenaps.com/prequilt-is-a-sleek-new-design-tool-for-quilters/); [Product Hunt reviews](https://www.producthunt.com/products/prequilt/reviews); [hunted.space "PreQuilt 3" launch](https://hunted.space/product/prequilt/launches/prequilt-3)
- Features: multiple grid layouts, solid fabric swatches from major manufacturers, over 100 common blocks, and a fabric calculator. — [Canadian Quilter](https://canadianquilter.com/?p=41598)
- Swatches from Kona, Art Gallery, Moda, Tilda "and lots more". An older article listed Kona and manufacturer prints as "future plans", so the library has grown over time. — [Suzy Quilts](https://suzyquilts.com/best-way-to-pick-fabric-for-a-quilt/); [While She Naps](https://whileshenaps.com/prequilt-is-a-sleek-new-design-tool-for-quilters/)
- A printed-fabric swatch importer exists (own fabric images). I could not confirm this from PreQuilt's own pages. — [Suzy Quilts](https://suzyquilts.com/?p=150778) (third-party text; treat as a lead)
- Limitation: the fabric calculator works only with quilts built inside PreQuilt, not with the coloring pages made by pattern designers. — [omidsaffari.com review (Arabic)](https://omidsaffari.com/ar/blog/prequilt-review-for-fabric-color-planning-ar)
- It is 2D only; no 3D drape was found. — [Canadian Quilter](https://canadianquilter.com/members-only-studio/visualize-your-next-quilt-with-prequilt)

**Quiltler (quiltler.com, plus iOS apps Quiltler / Quiltler 2 / Quiltler 3)**
- An online quilt designer that runs in the browser with "no account, card or download" to start. Photograph a print on your phone and it is placed on the quilt "at its real scale". A free account keeps one saved quilt and ten fabric photos (some of its pages say five). — [quiltler.com](https://quiltler.com/); [Quiltler online designer](https://quiltler.com/online-quilt-designer); [Quiltler Terms](https://quiltler.com/terms); [Quiltler beginners page](https://quiltler.com/quilt-design-software-for-beginners)

- It publishes SEO comparison pages ("Quiltler vs PreQuilt", "Free quilt design software: what 8 tools really offer", "Best quilt design apps 2026"). This shows it is actively competing for search traffic. — [Quiltler vs PreQuilt](https://quiltler.com/quiltler-vs-prequilt); [Free software page](https://quiltler.com/quilt-design-software-free); [Best apps 2026](https://quiltler.com/best-quilt-design-apps)
- iOS: Quiltler 3 is "Quilt Design App, Fabric Stash & Quilting Calculator". Its App Store listing says "all app functionality requires a subscription or trial" (7-day trial), which conflicts with the web free tier. — [App Store Quiltler 3](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6755831082); [Quiltler 2](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1495594391)

**NiftyFifty (niftyfiftyquilting.com): free, the closest feature match to the owner's app**

**Yard (quilt-yard.design / make.quilt-yard.design)**

**QuiltWall (humannpc.pages.dev/quiltwall): browser plus desktop**

**Swatchit (iOS plus swatchit.app web)**

### Inferences
- The market splits into four groups: (a) general freemium designers (PreQuilt, Quiltler, CINCHQuilt, QuiltWall, Swatchit), (b) free content-plus-tool sites (NiftyFifty, Yard, QuiltButler, Plan a quilt), (c) vendor-locked planners that exist to sell patterns and kits (Quiltster), and (d) single-purpose calculators and AI image toys.
- Many new entrants appeared in 2025–2026: Quiltler 3, Swatchit, QuiltFit and Quiltry all have high App Store IDs (676…/677… range), and NiftyFifty and Quiltler publish "2026" comparison pages. This suggests the niche is getting competitive, with SEO as the battleground.
- Older browser tools (Threadbias, PatternJam, MyWebQuilter, Palette Builder) seem to have stalled. Quilters want these tools, but small web tools have struggled to last.

- No Reddit r/quilting threads came up in search, so community opinion is thin. Product Hunt reviews exist for PreQuilt, but their content was not visible.

- Launch dates are unknown for Quiltler web, NiftyFifty, CINCHQuilt, QuiltButler, Yard, QuiltWall and Swatchit. Languages: every tool found appears to be English-only. No Portuguese or Spanish quilt designer was found (I did not search for one specifically).

## Which tools offer 3D or realistic previews, own fabric photos, cutting charts, printable real-size templates, offline use?

### Takeaway
No web tool found simulates a quilt draped on a bed or table in 3D. The nearest are Swatchit's AI "lifestyle scenes" (a photorealistic flat image) and Skopos's non-quilt 3D furniture tool. Using your own fabric photos is common (PreQuilt, Quiltler, QuiltWall, Quiltster, Quilt Wizard). Cutting charts are common in the newer tools. True-size printable templates are rare: NiftyFifty's free Pattern Designer is the main one, plus WaveSpeed's AI chart PDFs. Real offline use was not found in any browser tool. The "photo stays on your device" claims (WaveSpeed) are the closest.

## What are the pricing and business models?

### Takeaway
Freemium subscriptions of about $50–$100 a year are standard (PreQuilt Basic about $50/year, Quiltler $49.99/year, CINCHQuilt $79/year, Quiltster $99/year). The free tier usually allows 1–3 saved designs, and cutting instructions or saving are paywalled. Other models: one-time unlocks (Quiltler $4.99 per quilt, QuiltButler pattern library), plans for pattern designers and marketplaces (PreQuilt "Market", Quiltster marketplace, Quiltler embeds for fabric companies), and fully free content sites (NiftyFifty, Yard, Plan a quilt).

### Cited Findings
- PreQuilt: a free demo/"Forever Free" account (design and save one quilt; only solids). Paid plans come in two flavors, Basic and Market (Market is for pattern designers who sell their work), billed monthly or yearly. ### Inferences
- The going price for a hobbyist web designer is about $50/year (about R$ 270–300/year at rough current exchange rates, which is my estimate, not sourced). The usual gates are saved designs, printed fabrics and cutting instructions.
- Revenue beyond subscriptions comes from pattern designers (PreQuilt Market, Quiltler Designer) and fabric companies or shops (Quiltler embeds, Quiltster kits). A free tool with excellent PDFs could later sell to pattern designers or shops rather than to hobby quilters.

### Gaps

- No business-model information for NiftyFifty or Yard (free; how they earn is unknown).

## How does the owner's app compare?

### Takeaway
The owner's app is the only tool found that combines: a cloth-physics 3D drape on a bed or table, symmetry editing, fussy cutting, a quilting-stitch preview, real-size template PDFs, metric units and fully offline single-file use, free. Its weak spots against competitors are a small block library (27 against 100+ to 1,400+), no manufacturer fabric catalogues (Kona, Moda and so on, which PreQuilt, Swatchit and Quiltster use), no accounts, sync or sharing live yet, no pattern library or marketplace, and English/inch-centric search habits in this market (it is metric).

### Cited Findings
- Block library sizes: PreQuilt 100+, QuiltWall 200+, NiftyFifty 1,400+, PatternJam 24 (2015). — [Canadian Quilter](https://canadianquilter.com/?p=41598); [QuiltWall](https://humannpc.pages.dev/quiltwall/); [NiftyFifty](https://niftyfiftyquilting.com/quilt-designer); [KCPW](https://kcpw.org/local-news/2015-08-25/the-bottom-line-pattern-jam-taking-quilting-online/)
- Manufacturer swatches: PreQuilt (Kona, Art Gallery, Moda, Tilda), Swatchit (Kona name matching), Threadbias (Moda, historical). — [Suzy Quilts](https://suzyquilts.com/best-way-to-pick-fabric-for-a-quilt/); [Swatchit](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6760553382); [Moda blog](https://bog.modafabrics.com/node/26176)
- Real-size templates plus a cutting chart exist free at NiftyFifty (the closest overlap). — [NiftyFifty](https://niftyfiftyquilting.com/pattern-designer)
- The only "realistic setting" previews among quilt tools are Swatchit's AI scenes; no 3D cloth drape was found. — [mergeek.com](https://mergeek.com/latest/avyg8mL7q9OWR4pY)

### Inferences
- Strongest pitch: "see it on your bed before you cut", plus true-size templates in mm, free and offline. Each of these is either unique or rare among the tools found.
- Biggest threats: NiftyFifty (free, a huge library, PDFs) and Quiltler (polished freemium, phone-photo fabrics at real scale, aggressive SEO).
- The quick wins competitors already have: a visible "add your own fabric photo" entry point (the owner's app has the feature but its buttons are hidden), share links, and manufacturer solid palettes such as Kona.

### Gaps
- No hands-on test of any competitor was possible, so quality comparisons (ease of use, accuracy of cutting maths, PDF quality) are not verified.
- No Brazilian or Portuguese-language competitor was found. A targeted search ("programa para desenhar patchwork online") was not done within the search budget.
