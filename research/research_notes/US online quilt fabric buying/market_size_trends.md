# US Quilting Fabric Market: Size, Quilters, Online vs In-Store, Recent Shocks

Research date: 2026-11-07. Method note: page fetching was blocked in this session (craftindustryalliance.org, fabshopnet.com and gripsintelligence.com would not resolve), so the findings below come from search-engine summaries of those pages, not from reading the pages in full. Numbers should be checked against the linked pages before they are quoted in public. None of the market figures here are audited: they are survey projections (Premier Needle Arts / Quilting in America), analyst models (IBISWorld) or traffic-panel estimates (Grips Intelligence, ZoomInfo and similar). The one exception is Joann's sales, which come from court filings.

## 1. How big is the US quilting market, and how many quilters are there?

### Takeaway
The US quilting market is estimated at about **$4.5 billion a year (2025 Quilting Trends Survey, Premier Needle Arts)**, with **9–11 million active quilters**. Both numbers have been roughly flat for a decade. The older Quilting in America series (2010–2017) put the market at $3.6–3.8 billion, so nominal growth has been slow. There is no published fabric-only total in the sources found.

### Cited Findings
- **2025 survey:** market of **$4.5 billion**, below the **$4.958 billion** that the 2024 survey had projected for 2025. This is a projection from a survey of quilters, not audited sales. — [Craft Industry Alliance, Quilting Trends Survey Results 2025](https://craftindustryalliance.org/the-size-of-the-quilting-market-quilting-trends-survey-results-2025/)
- **June 2025 Premier Needle Arts presentation:** **9–11 million active quilters**, "a consistent number for the past 10 years". — [Today's Quilting Trends, June 2025 (PDF, fabshopnet.com)](https://fabshopnet.com/downloads/Todays-Quilting-Trends-June2025.pdf)
- **2020 survey:** **$4.2 billion** (reported Feb 2021), up from **$4.1 billion in 2018** and **$3.8 billion in 2014**. — [Craft Industry Alliance, Quilting Trends Survey Results 2020](https://craftindustryalliance.org/quilting-trends-survey-results-2020/)
- **2022 survey:** run by Premier Needle Arts. Just over 1 million people were invited and **more than 26,000 completed it**. Its summary reports "steady annual growth" and that **84% of quilters spend the same or more time quilting** than before. The search summaries did not give a 2022 dollar total. — [Craft Industry Alliance, 2022 results](https://craftindustryalliance.org/the-size-of-the-quilting-market-quilting-trends-survey-results-2022/)
- **Survey sizes:** the Premier survey has about 30,000 respondents, against about 2,000 for Quilting in America. — [Search summary of Craft Industry Alliance tag page](https://craftindustryalliance.org/tag/survey/)
- **Quilting in America 2010 (F+W / Quilts Inc.):** **16.38 million households (14%)** had an active quilter, about **21.3 million quilters**, a **$3.58 billion** market, and **$219 average spend per quilting household** (up 27% from 2006). — [QIA 2010 PDF](https://www.longarmuniversity.com/PDFs/WA-Legis/QuiltingInAmerica-2010.pdf); [blog summary of QIA 2010](https://desertlongarm.blogspot.com/2011/08/quilting-in-america-2010-survey-shows.html)
- **Quilting in America 2014:** **$3.76 billion** a year. — [QIA summary 2014 (PDF)](https://fabshopnet.com/wp-content/uploads/downloads/QIA_summary_2014.pdf)
- **Quilting in America 2017:** **$3.7 billion**. **72.2% of spending came from "dedicated quilters"**, a small group of heavy buyers. — [Search summary citing QIA 2017](https://whileshenaps.com/?p=25980)
- **Broader retail category (IBISWorld, analyst model):** "Fabric, Craft & Sewing Supplies Stores" in the US took in **$5.1 billion in 2025** (page updated April 2025) and about **$5.2 billion in 2026** (+1.5%). Fabrics is the largest segment, and Michaels is the largest company. IBISWorld counts the businesses as **38,137 (2025)** on one page and **31,349 (2026)** on another, and puts employment at **62,944 (2026)**. — [IBISWorld market size](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/market-size/fabric-craft-sewing-supply-stores/1081/); [IBISWorld number of businesses](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/number-of-businesses/fabric-craft-sewing-supplies-stores/1081)
- **IBISWorld "Online Hobby & Craft Supplies Sales" (all crafts, not just quilting):** **$25.3 billion in 2026**, +1.4%. — [IBISWorld online hobby & craft supplies](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/market-research-reports/online-hobby-craft-supplies-sales-industry/)

### Inferences
- The quilter count of 21 million in 2010 and 9–11 million today come from different surveys with different definitions. The 2010 figure counts any quilter in a household; Premier counts "active" quilters. The gap does not show that half of all quilters stopped.
- After inflation, the market has probably shrunk: $3.58 billion in 2010 to $4.5 billion in 2025 is about +26% in dollars, while US prices rose about 45–50% over that period. (The inflation figure is my general knowledge, not from a source found here.)
- Spending is concentrated: roughly 70% comes from dedicated quilters (2017). A product for heavy quilters reaches most of the money.
- Fabric is probably the largest share of the $4.5 billion, but no source found gives the split.

### Gaps
- No fabric-only spending figure was found for any year, and no fabric-versus-machines-versus-notions breakdown for the Premier surveys.
- No Quilting in America edition after 2017 turned up. The series seems to have been replaced by the Premier Needle Arts surveys.
- Average spend per quilter in 2024–2025 was not found in the search summaries.
- Statista figures were not reached.

## 2. Online versus local quilt shops versus big-box stores: shares and change since 2020

### Takeaway
In the 2025 survey, quilters said they **prefer local quilt shops for fabric and thread (51%) over online (22%)**. For quilting supplies in general it was **64% local against 13% online**, and the online preference **fell** compared with 2024 (61% local, 16% online). These are stated preferences from enthusiast survey respondents, not measured shares of sales. One commercial report puts online at **about 38.6% of quilting-fabric revenue**, which points to a much larger real online share than the surveys suggest.

### Cited Findings
- **2025 survey, fabric and thread:** **51% prefer a local shop, 22% online.** — [TCR Crafts summary of the 2025 survey](https://tcrcrafts.com/quilting-statistics); [Craft Industry Alliance 2025](https://craftindustryalliance.org/the-size-of-the-quilting-market-quilting-trends-survey-results-2025/)
- **2025 survey, all supplies:** **64% prefer a local shop (up 3 points), 13% online.** — [Craft Industry Alliance 2025](https://craftindustryalliance.org/the-size-of-the-quilting-market-quilting-trends-survey-results-2025/)
- **2024 survey, all supplies:** **61% local, 16% online.** — [Crafted Blog quilting statistics](https://craftedblog.com/quilting-statistics/)
- **2022 survey:** about **46% of quilters search online every day** for quilting products and education. — [Craft Industry Alliance 2022](https://craftindustryalliance.org/the-size-of-the-quilting-market-quilting-trends-survey-results-2022/)
- **Commercial estimate, not from the surveys:** online stores have **about 38.6% of quilting-fabric revenue in 2025**. This is a model with an unclear method and a global scope. — [Dataintelo quilting fabric market report](https://dataintelo.com/report/global-quilting-fabric-market)
- **Big-box stores:** Joann had about 800 stores and sold **about $2 billion in FY2024** (court documents). Its sales fell from **$2.76 billion to $2.22 billion between 2021 and 2023**, and part of the reason given was the end of the COVID crafting boom. — [Digital Commerce 360, Jan 2025](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/01/17/joann-files-for-bankruptcy-second-time-within-a-year/amp/); [Bondoro, Joann](https://bondoro.com/joann/)

### Inferences
- The surveys reach engaged quilters through shop and industry mailing lists, so they probably overstate how much quilters prefer local shops. The real online share of fabric dollars is likely between the 22% preference figure and the roughly 39% modelled figure. That is an estimate, not a measured number.
- The trend since 2020: COVID pushed online buying up in 2020–2021, then demand fell back. In the 2024→2025 surveys the online preference declined slightly. There are no sales-weighted channel figures for any year.
- Joann's roughly $2 billion of sales included much more than quilting cotton (crafts, apparel fabric, seasonal goods), so it cannot be compared directly with the $4.5 billion quilting figure.

### Gaps
- No measured, dollar-weighted split by channel (online / local shop / big box) was found for any year.
- No source found for the shares of Hobby Lobby or Walmart in quilting fabric.
- No published survey question asks Joann's former customers where they now buy.

## 3. Joann's bankruptcy and closure (2024–2025): where did its customers go?

### Takeaway
Joann filed for bankruptcy in **March 2024** and again on **January 15, 2025**. After the liquidator GA Group won the bid for its assets, **all of its roughly 800 stores closed by May 30, 2025**, and about **19,000 jobs** were lost. **Michaels bought the IP and private-label brands** (completed around June 5, 2025) but none of the stores. No data was found on where the customers went. Consumer guides point them to local quilt shops, Michaels, and online sellers (Missouri Star, Fat Quarter Shop, Hawthorne Supply, Etsy).

### Cited Findings
- **First Chapter 11, March 2024.** Reasons given: higher interest rates and falling sales after the COVID peak of 2021–2022. — [Digital Commerce 360, March 2024](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2024/03/19/joann-files-chapter-11-bankruptcy/)
- **Second filing, January 15, 2025, with $615.7 million of debt.** The first bankruptcy had removed $505 million of debt. Stock levels fell to 90% in summer 2024, the lowest in a decade. Inflation, competition and supply problems were also cited. — [Digital Commerce 360, Jan 2025](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/01/17/joann-files-for-bankruptcy-second-time-within-a-year/amp/); [CreditSights](https://know.creditsights.com/us-bankruptcy-joann-files-chapter-22-bankruptcy-in-delaware-just-eight-months-after-exiting-first-restructuring-gordon-brothers-to-lead-auction-with-liquidating-bid/)
- **February 2025: every store to close.** Joann had first planned to close about 500 of its 800 stores, then decided on all of them across 49 states after GA Group won the bid for substantially all of its assets. — [NPR, Feb 25 2025](https://www.npr.org/2025/02/25/nx-s1-5307907/joann-closing-stores-bankruptcy); [Axios, Feb 24 2025](https://www.axios.com/2025/02/24/joann-fabrics-closing-all-stores-bankruptcy)
- **Timeline of closures:** about 255 stores closed in April 2025 and the remaining 535 in May; the last stores closed **May 30, 2025**. — [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/michaels-acquires-joann-ip-private-labels/749927); [Wikiwand, Jo-Ann Fabrics](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Jo-Ann_Fabrics)
- **Michaels (through SVP Sewing Brands LLC) bought Joann's IP and private-label brands; no stores were included.** It was not known whether Michaels would use the Joann name. — [Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/features/mergers-and-acquisitions/michaels-buys-bankrupt-joanns-ip-and-private-label-brands); [Chain Store Age](https://chainstoreage.com/michaels-acquires-intellectual-property-private-label-brands-joann); [Store Brands](https://storebrands.com/michaels-acquires-joann-brands-expands-fabric-and-yarn-offerings)
- **Where shoppers were pointed:** local quilt shops, Missouri Star Quilt Co., Hawthorne Supply Co., Michaels (one guide lists fabric at $4/yard) and Etsy. Local shops carry mostly quilting cotton, cost more ("sticker shock"), and do not replace Joann's apparel and upholstery fabric. — [AOL](https://www.aol.com/lifestyle/joann-is-closing-its-doors-heres-where-to-buy-fabric-instead-184739459.html); [Des Moines magazine, Jul 2025](https://dsmmagazine.com/2025/07/02/sew-long-joann-fabric-stores-closure-creates-a-hard-to-patch-hole/); [Camas-Washougal Post-Record, Jun 2025](https://www.camaspostrecord.com/news/2025/jun/26/looking-for-fabric-since-joann-closed-there-are-options/); [NPR, Feb 28 2025](https://www.npr.org/2025/02/28/nx-s1-5308188/joann-closing-customers)
- **Industry advice to quilt shops on welcoming Joann's shoppers.** — [RBD blog, Mar 2025](https://www.rbdblog.com/2025/03/11/welcoming-joann-shoppers-part-2/)
- **Online shops marketing directly to former Joann customers.** — [Linda's Electric Quilters](https://lindas.com/pages/joann-alternative)
- **joann.com also closed.** — [Classic Modern Fabrics](https://classicmodernfabrics.com/blogs/news/a-letter-to-fabric-and-craft-lovers-joann-com-is-closing-here-s-what-you-need-to-know-and-where-to-shop-next)
- **Singer Sourcing reportedly bought some IP for $800,000.** This appeared in only one source and is unconfirmed. — [Elevenflo](https://elevenflo.com/blog/joann-2025-bankruptcy)

### Inferences
- Joann's closure took away the only national fabric chain and the cheapest way to buy in person. Price-sensitive and beginner quilters most likely moved to Michaels, Walmart and online stores; dedicated quilters moved to local shops. This is an inference from the guides; no data was found on it.
- Grips' online figures do not show a jump in online quilting sales in 2025 (see section 5): flat at 0–5% down. So Joann's customers did not obviously show up at the tracked online quilt shops.

### Gaps
- No data was found on where the customers went, in sales or surveys, and no earnings comment from Michaels or Hobby Lobby on fabric gains.
- Michaels' later plans for Joann brands and fabric departments in 2025–2026 were not found.

## 4. Number of independent quilt shops and the trend

### Takeaway
No reliable count exists. Estimates range from about **1,500 to 4,500**. The most-cited series (QuiltingHub's registry) shows a decline from **about 5,500 in 2010 to about 3,200** (an article from around 2023). Anecdotal reports describe closures continuing into 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **QuiltingHub owner Paul Johnson:** about **5,500 shops in 2010 → about 3,200** at the time of the article (around 2023, judging from the "about 900 days old" note). — [While She Naps, "How Quilt Market Could Invest in Its Own Revival…"](https://whileshenaps.com/how-quilt-market-could-invest-in-its-own-revival-by-catering-to-online-retailers/)
- **A blog estimate of 4,547 shops**, based on the tax code for quilt shops, with the note that closures over the past year mean there could be fewer. — [search summary, 2025 "where to shop" guides](https://www.freemotionquiltingadventures.com/2025/05/where-to-shop-now-that-joann-is-closed.html)
- **A commercial contact list of 4,490 "quilt shops" (Oct 28, 2025).** Not a verified census. — [Bytescraper](https://bytescraper.com/b2b-database/list-of-quilt-shops-in-united-states)
- **A directory site's "about 1,500 independent shops" in 2026.** Unverified. — [Nifty Fifty Quilting](https://niftyfiftyquilting.com/quilt-shops)
- **A statistics site:** "We couldn't find any credible count of independent quilt shops." — [QuiltGearHQ](https://quiltgearhq.com/quilting-statistics/)
- **Bloggers report a steady decline in brick-and-mortar shops over ten years.** — [Sherri Quilts A Lot, 2019](https://sherriquiltsalot.com/2019/05/22/the-grumpy-quilter-and-the-changing-face-of-the-quilt-world-part-one/)

### Inferences
- The direction is down, roughly 40% fewer shops between 2010 and the early 2020s by QuiltingHub's count. The range of current estimates is too wide to support a precise figure. Shops with a website and online sales are likely the ones that last, which blurs "local" and "online".

### Gaps
- No yearly count of openings and closures for 2024–2026 was found.

## 5. Online quilting e-commerce: estimates and forecasts

### Takeaway
Grips Intelligence, a traffic panel whose figures are estimates, puts **online quilting sales across the retailers it tracks at $287 million in 2025 (0–5% below 2024)** and forecasts a **20–50% fall in 2026**. Its "fabric and quilting supplies" category is $294 million for 2025, with a 5–10% fall forecast. **Fat Quarter Shop** is the largest online seller found, at roughly **$9–10 million a month**. **Connecting Threads** was about **$4.4 million in November 2025**. Revenue estimates for Missouri Star range from **about $48 million to $250 million a year**.

### Cited Findings
- **Grips, "quilting" category:** **$287 million in 2025**, 0–5% below 2024; **forecast 20–50% decline in 2026**; **66% of revenue on desktop, 34% on mobile**. — [Grips: quilting](https://gripsintelligence.com/insights/industries/arts-entertainment/quilting)
- **Grips, "fabric and quilting supplies":** **$294 million in 2025**; forecast **−5% to −10% in 2026**. — [Grips: fabric and quilting supplies](https://gripsintelligence.com/insights/industries/home-garden/fabric-and-quilting-supplies)
- **Grips, sewing and quilting supplies (a separate category page).** — [Grips: sewing and quilting supplies](https://gripsintelligence.com/insights/industries/arts-entertainment/sewing-and-quilting-supplies)
- **fatquartershop.com (Grips):** **$10.42 million in November 2025**, **$9.4 million in December 2025**, down 2.7% over the prior three months. — [Grips: fatquartershop.com](https://gripsintelligence.com/insights/retailers/fatquartershop.com)
- **Fat Quarter Shop (ZoomInfo):** **$29.2 million a year**. This conflicts with Grips, whose monthly figures add up to more than $100 million a year. — [ZoomInfo: Fat Quarter Shop](https://www.zoominfo.com/c/fat-quarter-shop-llc/351116030)
- **A competitor's blog claims $14.5 million a month for Fat Quarter Shop (July 2026).** No source is given. — [Linda's: Missouri Quilt Co vs Fat Quarter Shop](https://lindas.com/blogs/learn-and-love/missouri-quilt-co-vs-fat-quarter-shop)
- **connectingthreads.com (Grips):** **$4.43 million, 30,891 orders, 449,544 visits in November 2025**; **$2.20 million, 15,752 orders, 262,745 visits in December 2025**. That works out to an average order of about $140–143 (my calculation). No full-year figure was found; the ~$23.5 million for 2024 in the brief could not be checked. — [Grips: connectingthreads.com](https://gripsintelligence.com/insights/retailers/connectingthreads.com)
- **Missouri Star Quilt Co. revenue estimates:** ZoomInfo / Kona Equity **$48.1 million**; Growjo **$71.2 million**; LeadIQ **$100–250 million (Aug 2026)**; IncFact $10–100 million. None of these are filings. — [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/c/missouri-star-quilt-co/346066566); [Kona Equity](https://www.konaequity.com/company/missouri-star-quilt-company-4397877660/); [Growjo](https://growjo.com/company/Missouri_Star_Quilt_Co); [LeadIQ](https://leadiq.com/c/missouri-star-quilt-company/5a1d98b82300005c00875b61)
- **Missouri Star ownership:** in **October 2024**, LoveCrafts was bought by Creative Fiber Holdings, which has the same owners as Missouri Star. — [LoveCrafts announcement](https://www.lovecrafts.com/en-gb/c/article/lovecrafts-group-acquired); [CraftGossip, Sep 2024](https://knitting.craftgossip.com/missouri-quilt-company-to-acquire-lovecrafts/2024/09/30/)
- **Other Grips monthly snapshots:** AccuQuilt **$704,557 (Sep 2024)**; The Quilt Show **$365,286 (Sep 2024)**. — [Grips: accuquilt.com](https://gripsintelligence.com/insights/retailers/accuquilt.com); [Grips: thequiltshow.com](https://gripsintelligence.com/insights/retailers/thequiltshow.com)

### Inferences
- Grips' $287 million seems low next to Fat Quarter Shop alone at more than $100 million a year (Grips' own figures) plus Missouri Star at $48 million or more. The category likely covers only some sites, or puts the big sellers in other categories. It is best read as a trend signal (flat to down), not as the size of the online market.
- A rough cross-check: if online is 22–39% of a $4.5 billion market, online quilting spend would be about $1.0–1.75 billion. That is my calculation, it covers all supplies rather than just fabric, and it is uncertain.
- The 20–50% decline Grips forecasts for 2026 is a model output and is not backed by any reported events. Use it with caution.

### Gaps
- No audited revenue for any online quilt seller. Missouri Star, Fat Quarter Shop and Connecting Threads are private.
- Amazon, Etsy and Walmart.com fabric sales are not found anywhere.
- No independent forecast of online quilting sales from Statista or eMarketer was found.

## 6. Tariffs and prices for cotton quilting fabric, 2025–2026

### Takeaway
Most quality quilting cotton sold by US fabric companies is **printed in South Korea and Japan** and imported, so the **2025 tariffs raised wholesale costs**. A shop owner reports **costs up 10–25% since April 2025**, passed on to customers. Licensed prints rose about 14% (cotton) and 18% (fleece) at one shop. Online retail prices rose and then settled around **$13+ a yard with heavy discounting** (December 2025). No announcement of a price increase from the fabric makers (Moda, Riley Blake) was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Fine quilting cottons are printed mostly in South Korea and Japan**, so tariffs raise the importers' prices. Updated after three months, Dec 2025. — [Barbara Brackman, "Tariff Updates After Three Months," Dec 2025](https://barbarabrackman.blogspot.com/2025/12/tariff-updates-after-three-months.html)
- **A local fabric store:** costs rose **about 10–25% since early 2025** (from the April 2, 2025 tariff announcement) and were passed on to customers. Oct 2026. — [The Confident Stitch, Oct 27 2026](https://www.theconfidentstitch.com/2026/10/27/how-trumps-tariffs-have-affected-a-local-fabric-store/)
- **NFL licensed fabric wholesale prices up more than 14% (cotton) and 18% (fleece).** One retailer's account. — [Mary Jo's Fabrics](https://maryjofabrics.com/blogs/news/navigating-the-fabric-tariff-landscape-how-to-keep-your-crafting-passion-alive)
- **Online prices rose, then fell back to about $13+ a yard, with most fabric on sale.** Dec 2025. — [Barbara Brackman, Dec 2025](https://barbarabrackman.blogspot.com/2025/12/tariff-updates-after-three-months.html)
- **Quilting-community concern about tariffs, May 2025.** — [Sherri Quilts A Lot, "Quilting and the Tariffs," May 2025](https://sherriquiltsalot.com/2025/05/07/quilting-and-the-tariffs/)
- **Apparel context:** the average US tariff on apparel imports (HS 61–62) reached **35.1% in December 2025**, though the unit price of cotton apparel imports was nearly flat in 2025. — [Sheng Lu Fashion, Mar 2026](https://shenglufashion.com/2026/03/09/tariffs-impact-u-s-apparel-sourcing-and-trade-beyond-just-price-updated-march-2026/)

### Inferences
- Higher shelf prices and heavy online discounting together suggest weaker demand. That fits the flat or falling online sales and the $4.5 billion survey result coming in below the projection.

### Gaps
- No official price index for quilting cotton, and no statements from the fabric makers about prices.
- The specific tariff rates on South Korean and Japanese printed cotton were not confirmed.
- The search summary's claim that "cotton futures above 90 cents" did not match my knowledge of 2025 cotton prices, so it was left out as unverified.
